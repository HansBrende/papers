\section{Exact homogenization and its width obstruction}\label{sec:inflation}

It is natural to try to encode sheet-dependent coefficients inside
larger homogeneous blocks. The following transformation is exact,
but its module structure explains why it does not settle the
unrestricted-width problem.

\begin{proposition}[Rate-preserving inflation]\label{prop:inflation}
\formal{HomogeneousInflation.lean}{sheetCheck,inflatedCheck,sheetReindex,reindex_inflated,kernelEquiv,finrank_kernel_inflated,rate_cross_product}
On a finite quotient $Q$ of order $q$, allow coefficients indexed by
the destination sheet:
\[
 (M_Cx)_{v,t}=x_{v,t}-\sum_{e=(u,v,g)}C_e(t)x_{u,g^{-1}t}.
\]
There is a homogeneous check on the same external voltage support,
with each internal width multiplied by $q$, that is permutation
equivalent to $q$ copies of $M_C$. Its normalized nullity is unchanged.
\end{proposition}
\begin{outline}
The formalization takes $W=\bigoplus_vW_v$ and extends every
$C_e(t)$ by zero from its source type to its target type. Thus one
sheet is a vector in $W$, and the two checks below are exactly
\lean{sheetCheck} and \lean{inflatedCheck} on $W$-valued functions.
Replace $W_v$ by $\widetilde W_v=\bigoplus_{t\in Q}W_v$ and set
$(D_ez)_t=C_e(t)z_{g^{-1}t}$. The homogeneous check is
$\widetilde M=I-\sum_e D_e\otimes\rho_Q(g_e)$.
Its coordinates $x_{v,x,t}$ have external sheet $x$ and internal
sheet $t$, and a dependency simultaneously replaces these by
$g^{-1}x,g^{-1}t$. Therefore $h=x^{-1}t$ is invariant.
For each $h\in Q$ put $y^h_{v,t}=x_{v,th^{-1},t}$.
This bijective reindexing turns the check into the original equations
for $y^h$, independently for each $h$.

The formal kernel equivalence is
$\ker\widetilde M\cong\prod_{h\in Q}\ker M_C$.
Mathlib's dimension formulas therefore give
$\dim\ker\widetilde M=q\dim\ker M_C$, while the ambient
dimensions are respectively $Bq^2$ and $Bq$. The Lean rate identity
is stated by cross multiplication, so it also holds at zero width;
dividing by the positive ambient dimensions gives the stated rate
equality. Neither commutativity of $Q$ nor distinct voltages is assumed.
\end{outline}

For a linear subcode $\mathcal C\le\ker M_C$, the same coordinate
permutation takes $\prod_{h\in Q}\mathcal C$ into the inflated
kernel. Its dimension is $q\dim\mathcal C$, so it too preserves rate.

\begin{proposition}[One large absolutely simple factor]
\label{prop:large-factor}
\notformalized
There is a fixed paired-separated template on an actual chain
\eqref{eq:chain} for which the inflation in \cref{prop:inflation}
produces a coefficient module that remains simple after every field
extension, of dimension $2q_i\to\infty$.
Nevertheless every storage code on the underlying two-type relation
has rate at most one half for equal coordinate alphabets.
\end{proposition}
\begin{informalproof}
Take $\Gamma=\langle a\rangle\subset\mathcal G=\Z_p\times\Z_p$
by $a\mapsto(1,0)$, with $U_i=p^i\Z_p\times p^i\Z_p$.
Then $Q_i=C_{p^i}$. Use two types $0,1$ and four triples
\[
 (0,1,1),\quad(1,0,1),\quad(0,1,a),\quad(0,1,a^2).
\]
Their incidence edges stay in the disjoint classes
$\{L_0,R_1\}$ and $\{L_1,R_0\}$, so every matched pair is
disconnected in the infinite and finite lifts.

For scalar coordinates over $\F_p$, choose destination-sheet
coefficients $1,1,1,\mathbf1_{t=1}$ in that order. On the inflated
internal space $Z_i=\F_p^{Q_i}$, let $P e_t=e_{at}$ and let $F$
project onto the identity sheet. The four internal maps are
$I,I,P,FP^2$. Include the two vertex projectors in their algebra.
The cross identities put $P$ and $FP^2$ in one diagonal corner.
Since $P^{q_i}=I$, that corner also contains $P^{-2}$ and therefore
$F$. For any sheets $x,y$, powers of $P$ give
$P_xFP_y^{-1}=E_{xy}$, the corresponding matrix unit.
The cross identities transfer these units to all four type corners.
Consequently the algebra is
$\operatorname{Mat}_{2q_i}(\F_p)$, and remains the full matrix
algebra after any field extension. Its natural module is simple:
a full endomorphism algebra can send any nonzero vector to any
specified vector. Thus $\alpha_i(k)=1$ eventually for every fixed $k$.

Finally each type is recoverable from the other type alone.
Projection onto either full type is injective on any storage code.
With $q_i$ coordinates per type and common alphabet $\Sigma$,
$|\mathcal C|\le|\Sigma|^{q_i}$. The same argument applies to the
inflated block alphabet. No rate attainment by the displayed check
is asserted.
\end{informalproof}

The example refutes automatic small-factor mass after homogenization.
It does not refute a possible all-width half-rate theorem. In
particular, the contrapositive of \cref{cor:factor-excess} provides
a necessary algebraic escape condition, not a sufficient construction.
