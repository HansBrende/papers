\section{Templates, quotients, and checks}\label{sec:model}

Fix a prime $p$ and a finitely generated abstract free group $\Gamma$.
A finite directed voltage template consists of a finite nonempty set
$V$ of types and a finite set $E$ of triples $e=(u,v,g)$ with
$u,v\in V$ and $g\in\Gamma$. The triple permits a type-$u$ input for
a type-$v$ receiver. Equal triples are collected before assigning
coefficients. Words that represent different elements of $\Gamma$
remain separate even if they later coincide in a quotient.

For a quotient $q:\Gamma\twoheadrightarrow Q$, the incidence lift
$I_Q$ has ports $L_{(v,x)},R_{(v,x)}$ for $v\in V,x\in Q$, with edges
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lift}
 L_{(u,x)}\;--\;R_{(v,q(g)x)}\qquad ((u,v,g)\in E).
\end{equation}
This graph is undirected for connectivity and distance; the recovery
relation itself need not be symmetric. The two ports of $(v,x)$ are
\emph{matched}. The infinite lift $I_\Gamma$ uses the identity quotient.
Common right multiplication of sheets is a graph automorphism.
Our separation hypothesis is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:separation}
 L_{(v,1)}\text{ and }R_{(v,1)}\text{ lie in different components of }
 I_\Gamma\quad\text{for every }v\in V.
\end{equation}
It then holds at every sheet. The literal template is retained even
when selected coefficients vanish or cancel.

Give type $v$ a finite-dimensional space $W_v$ over a field $K$,
put $w_v=\dim_K W_v$ and $B=\sum_vw_v>0$, and choose
$C_e:W_u\to W_v$ for every $e=(u,v,g)$.
Let $T_e$ be its extension by zero to $W=\bigoplus_vW_v$.
With $\rho_Q(g)e_x=e_{q(g)x}$, define the homogeneous check
\begin{equation}\label{eq:check}
 M_Q=I-\sum_{e\in E}T_e\otimes\rho_Q(g_e),\qquad
 (M_Qx)_{v,y}=x_{v,y}-\sum_{e=(u,v,g)}C_e x_{u,q(g)^{-1}y}.
\end{equation}
Its normalized nullity is
$r_Q=\dim_K\ker M_Q/(B|Q|)$.
Every linear subcode of this kernel has at most this rate; over finite
$K$ this agrees with logarithmic cardinality normalized by $B|Q|$.
Homogeneity means that $C_e$ does not depend on the sheet $y$.

A linear storage space with deterministic recovery admits linear
recovery maps: on the attained parent projection define the map taking
a parent tuple to its determined coordinate, then extend it linearly
by a basis. This observation does not force homogeneous choices when
different sheets originally have different maps. Homogeneity is a
separate hypothesis of the cover theorems.

\paragraph{The actual chain.}
For the rank limits, fix a faithful embedding of $\Gamma$ in a
free-by-$\Z_p$ pro-$p$ group $\mathcal G$. This means that
$\mathcal G$ has a closed normal free pro-$p$ subgroup with quotient
$\Z_p$. Fix descending normal open subgroups $U_i$ with
$\bigcap_iU_i=\{1\}$ in $\mathcal G$, and put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:chain}
 H_i=\Gamma\cap U_i,\qquad Q_i=\Gamma/H_i,\qquad q_i=|Q_i|.
\end{equation}
The normalization is $q_i$, not $|\mathcal G/U_i|$ unless the two
quotients coincide. No cofinality in the separate pro-$p$ completion
of $\Gamma$ is assumed. The natural maps $Q_j\to Q_i$, $j\ge i$,
are surjective and have $p$-group kernels.

The prime, template, embedding, and chain stay fixed in every cover
limit below. Widths are fixed through \cref{sec:uniformity} and may
grow only under the explicit hypotheses in \cref{sec:factors,sec:weighted}.
Zero-width types may be omitted. When nonidentity within-type voltages
occur, an early quotient may collapse one to $1$; then the matrix is
still a check but need not describe recovery without self-input.
Separation excludes identity within-type voltages, and the finite
template and trivial intersection ensure that no such collapse occurs
at sufficiently late levels.

\paragraph{One filtration fact used throughout.}
If a linear map $T$ preserves a finite filtration
$0=F_0\subset F_1\subset\cdots\subset F_m=X$, and $T_j$ is its
map on $F_j/F_{j-1}$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:filtered-kernel}
 \dim\ker T\le\sum_{j=1}^m\dim\ker T_j.
\end{equation}
Indeed the map from
$(\ker T\cap F_j)/(\ker T\cap F_{j-1})$ to $F_j/F_{j-1}$
is injective and lands in $\ker T_j$. Sum dimensions.
The filtration need not split, and quotient kernel vectors need not lift.
