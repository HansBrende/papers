\section{Use in the transposition program}\label{sec:applications}

We keep the series' notation: $d$ is the matrix dimension,
$b(d)=\max\{1,\lceil\log_2d\rceil\}$, and $L=d^2b(d)$ is the input
bit-length. The question (T) asks for a deterministic multitape
transposer taking $O(L)$ time \cite{FirstPaper2026}. In the nats used
here, the full input capacity is $L\log 2$.

\paragraph{What an impossibility application must establish.}
An application first chooses a distribution on the same input family
throughout, selects coordinates, and proves a lower bound of the form
\eqref{eq:initial-deficit}. \Cref{lem:initial} permits a paid reveal
and conditioning on the unselected input without assuming independence
under that distribution. It must then provide a decomposable parent
relation and actual helper values completing or partially completing
the selected recoveries. The occurrence cover must preserve the local
contexts \eqref{eq:actual-context}.

For one chosen reveal $Q$, all remaining charges must be evaluated
under that same distribution and common context. To contradict
\cref{thm:information}, it would suffice to prove
\begin{equation}
2\delta+2J_Q+\Phi_Q+D_Q+E_Q<B+S_Q.
\label{eq:paid-margin}
\end{equation}
Here the strict upper bound must follow from the assumed machine
class, not be supplied as an independent certificate assumption. A
uniform lower bound on the positive gap in
\eqref{eq:paid-margin}, as $d$ grows, is a remaining target. No such
gap for unrestricted native tape computations is established in this
memo.

\paragraph{What a construction must account for.}
The same inequality is necessary for any compatible recovery scheme.
Keeping earlier information in a new encoding is allowed. It can
preserve semantic retention even when literal variable containment
fails. If information is discarded, the helper-specific decrease is
charged; if recovery is incomplete, $E_Q$ is charged. Merely changing
record names does not remove the occurrence or entropy obligations.

Records at different times are distinct observations, even if stored in
the same tape cell. An equality or decoder between those observations
may establish an entropy relation, but physical location alone does
not. Nor may the contexts be enlarged by inserting earlier targets or
whole tape blocks without proving that the revised recovery relation
and occurrence cover meet their respective hypotheses. The memo's
decoder lemmas certify information identities, not free computation.

\paragraph{Remaining questions.}
Three concrete tasks would establish further utility. First, identify
a family of actual computations or recovery schemes for which the
decomposition and occurrence-cover conditions can be obtained with
quantitative bounds on all charges. Second, exhibit a substantial
improvement over the applicable older bounds after their reveal and
cover choices have also been optimized; the finite controls in
\cref{sec:sharpness} compare individual charges only. Third, find
conditions under which the sharper $D_Q$ can be bounded without solving
the full joint information problem. A useful native instance of
\eqref{eq:paid-margin} would advance the impossibility program;
compatible instances instead constrain where a construction can
retain or replenish the required information. The general transposition
question remains open.
