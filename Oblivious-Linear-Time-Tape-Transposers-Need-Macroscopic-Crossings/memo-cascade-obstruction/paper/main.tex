\begin{abstract}
The five implemented cascade schedules accompanying \emph{Oblivious
Linear-Time Tape Transposers Need Macroscopic Crossings} satisfy the
regular-recursion obstruction of \emph{Local Coding Barriers and the Limits
of Tape Geometry for Matrix Transposition}, Theorem~7.5. We verify its
hypotheses on the complete published schedules, including preparation,
positioning, recursive stages and output. For each fixed work alphabet,
every sufficiently large dyadic instance has controller-bit cost plus
correctness deficit at least a positive constant times its input length.
The conclusion allows arbitrary nonlinear updates at every visit and holds
both for bit entries and for canonical logarithmic entries. The proof is
asymptotic; it gives no numerical exclusion threshold. The imported
obstruction is Lean-verified. The canonical-width C5 exclusion is also
formalized, including its complete stage clock, arbitrary local-rule
compiler, regularity and real-deficit bound. Its declarative Lean schedule
is matched to the Rust source by a written audit and finite trace
comparisons. The other four schedule applications, the bit-to-word
simulation and the entry-information extension have written proofs.
\end{abstract}
