\begin{abstract}
We prove coding and head-motion bounds for finite information-access models and for matrix
transposition on deterministic multitape Turing machines, using the native
model of \emph{Matrix Transposition and the Tape Coding Conjecture}. For local
coding, we obtain adaptive original-row separator bounds with optimal universal
deficit and helper coefficients over finite instances; exact distance bounds
for fixed two-coordinate Boolean packets after arbitrary bijective row
recoding; and a
$b\floor{d^2/4}$ lower bound on helper entropy plus input entropy deficit for a
specified two-stage architecture. Here $d$ is the matrix dimension and $b$ the
entry width. The packet bound extends to correlated inputs and helper-selected
global bijections with local inverses. With bounded stored-row degree, inverse
support and inverse reuse, it excludes fixed fractional savings when input
deficit plus helper entropy is $o(d^2b)$; an $O(db)$ information budget gives
the explicit error $O(d^2b/\log d)$. For adaptive two-coordinate queries, a
leafwise forest condition and small interval-recovery states give a near-full
answer bound, with an explicit $O(d^2b/\log\log d)$ error under row-sized
information budgets. One fixed native machine computes an expander shear in
linear time from raw input and blank work tapes. These banks have bounded
inverse supports and zero forward column radius, yet require linear-size
interval-recovery budgets in expectation even after randomized helper-selected
row orders and within-row bijections, when input deficit plus helper mutual
information is sublinear.
For head motion, we bound time outside
locally affine regions and exclude specified scan palettes and regular
contracting recursions.
We also construct, for every native machine, a fixed wrapper with additive
$O(d^2b)$ overhead on canonical inputs whose complete runs satisfy the original
zero-slack cut inequality. Under a linear-time bound, its normalized head limits
satisfy the full $M^*$ conditions. Preparation, motion, cleanup and handoff are
all charged. These geometric conditions can therefore be supplied independently
of the computation that follows the prefix. The stated results are formalized in
Lean. Unrestricted linear-time transposition and integer multiplication remain
unresolved here.
\end{abstract}
