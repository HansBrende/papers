\begin{abstract}
We prove six families of restrictions and constructions for matrix
transposition on deterministic multitape Turing machines, using the model of
\emph{Matrix Transposition and the Tape Coding Conjecture}. For local coding,
we obtain adaptive original-row separator bounds with sharp deficit and helper
coefficients; near-full broadcast bounds for all-input correctness after
bijective nonlinear row recoding, when fixed coordinate-XOR packets follow a
bounded-degree row graph; and a
$b\floor{d^2/4}$ lower bound on helper entropy plus input entropy deficit for a
specified two-stage architecture. Here $d$ is the matrix dimension and $b$ the
entry width. For head motion, we bound time outside locally affine regions and
exclude specified scan palettes and regular contracting recursions.
We also construct, for every native machine, a fixed wrapper with additive
$O(d^2b)$ overhead on canonical inputs whose complete runs satisfy the original
zero-slack cut inequality. Under a linear-time bound, its normalized head limits
satisfy the full $M^*$ conditions. Preparation, motion, cleanup and handoff are
all charged. These geometric conditions can therefore be supplied independently
of the computation that follows the prefix. All six families are formalized in
Lean. Unrestricted linear-time transposition and integer multiplication remain
unresolved here.
\end{abstract}
