\begin{quote}\small
\textbf{Contributions.} Hans Brende chose the questions and directed the research. Gemini~4 Argon, a large language
model developed by Google DeepMind, found the two rational codes of \cref{sec:fields} by computer search, gave the first
Lean proof of the code over the fields in which $1008\ne0$, proposed the blow-up of \cref{sec:blowup} in a coset form,
found the parity-coset rules of \cref{sec:twoclass} in closed form for every $d=4k$, and the other partitions of
\cref{rem:parity-numerical}, gave a first Lean proof at $d=8,12,16$ and the rank count on the even anti-diagonals in the
proof of \cref{thm:parity-all}, and proved, in related one-hop models, that linear advice of rank $q$ costs at most
$q$ dimensions. Claude Opus~5.5, a
large language model developed by Anthropic, checked these results, identified the blow-up as a tensor construction and
worked out what it says about the parent paper's problems, proved the remaining results of \cref{sec:advice}, completed the
proof of \cref{thm:parity-all}, wrote the Lean library of this memo, and wrote the text. This memo extends the parent paper~\cite{SeventeenthPaper2026} and
modifies neither the paper nor its formalization.
\end{quote}
