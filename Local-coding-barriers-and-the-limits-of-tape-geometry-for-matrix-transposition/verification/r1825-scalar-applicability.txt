3. Common scalar scale and bounded scalar witnesses X,E.
   OPEN: construct the actual finite scalar sets from the same matrix witnesses,
   put X in[-1,1] and E in[-W,W], and choose a common quarter-power scale.
   Exact commuting extraction approximates actual x0^(-1)x with radius
      xi=12800 B0^6 eta/r0,
      r0=eta^(2zeta)/(2*4^34), B0=4*4^34.
   Thus xi is of order eta^(1-2zeta), not eta. A coarser-scale conversion needs
   explicit covering, separation, and exponent losses.

4. X nonempty, separated, and log|X|<=(1-kappa)log(1/eta).
   OPEN for the actual scalar witness. Matrix packing and a globally large
   commuting image do not by themselves give this scalar interface.

5. Normalized scalar ball counts for X, exponent kappa.
   OPEN. The new bound is relative to |F|. Projection may collapse fibers and
   selection may retain a small fraction. A bound relative to N_eta(A) is not
   automatically a bound relative to |X|. The known commuting-image lower bound
   can be only a cube root of N_eta(A), up to power losses; multiplying by the
   resulting ratio N_eta(A)/N_eta(V) can cost roughly N_eta(A)^(2/3).
   That is not a justified arbitrarily small power loss.

6. Small additive doubling: 5 N_eta(X+X) <= W |X|.
   OPEN. Matrix product control does not equal scalar additive control in
   the determinant-one centralizer. Scalar affine coordinates sI+tg satisfy a
   determinant constraint; their multiplication is a bilinear operation on
   that constraint, not automatically scalar addition. Treat unipotent,
   elliptic, and real semisimple cases without an unproved eigenvalue gap.

7. E nonempty and normalized scalar ball counts, exponent2*kappa.
   OPEN. Global matrix distribution does not give the required distribution of
   the actual coefficient map. Parameter exponents can be reduced in advance,
   but a nonconcentration proof for the resulting map is still required.

8. Scalar growth transfer to a bounded actual matrix product.
   OPEN. Need an explicit representation of X+aX through bounded products of
   the same A, with all approximation and covering multiplicities accounted for.
   Fixed near-identity radius alpha does not make quadratic errors negligible
   at eta tending to zero. No uncharged fine-scale linearization is valid.

9. Upper envelope for the same product and family.
