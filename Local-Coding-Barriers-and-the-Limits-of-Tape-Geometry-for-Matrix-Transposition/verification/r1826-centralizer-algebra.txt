R1826 — EXACT CENTRALIZER ALGEBRA AND ACTUAL PRODUCT TRANSFER

Status: three local Lean modules,12 theorems. Development builds passed;
full verification is recorded separately in support-build.json.
These are supporting algebra/analysis lemmas, not a fourth main theorem family.
No novelty claim is made.

NOTATION
Use the existing real two-by-two matrix scalarAffine:
  U(s,t)=s I+t a,
where a is a fixed real matrix. Let T=tr(a), d=det(a), Delta=T^2-4d.
Let D=det(a g-g a)=det(pairSpanMatrix(a,g)). The equality for D is reused
from SL2SpanCertificate.pairSpanMatrix_det. Delta and D are DIFFERENT quantities.

EXACT FORMULAS — NO DIAGONALIZATION
1. U(s,t)U(p,q)=U(sp-tqd, sq+tp+tqT).
2. adj(U(s,t))=U(s+tT,-t).
3. tr(U(s,t))^2-4det(U(s,t))=t^2 Delta.
4. If det(U(s,t))=det(U(p,q))=1, write t_plus=sq+tp+tqT and
   t_minus=tp-sq. These are the actual affine coefficients of uv and uv^(-1),
   and
      t_plus^2+t_minus^2=2(t^2+q^2)+Delta*t^2*q^2.
   The general determinant-weighted identity is also proved.
5. For u=U(s,t), v=U(p,q) in SL2(R), and g in SL2(R),
      tr(v u^(-1) g v u g)=tr(v g v g)+D*t^2.
   The corresponding polynomial identity for arbitrary real matrices, with
   the two determinants explicit, is proved first. There is no division by
   Delta, no eigenbasis, and no positivity or nonzero assumption on Delta.
   In particular, the trace separation identity includes the unipotent case.

ACTUAL MATRIX WORDS AND THE ERROR COST
Let u',v' be actual matrices approximating u,v entrywise to error delta>=0.
Assume all entries of u,v,u',v',g have absolute value at most R>=1.
Then
  |tr(v' u'^(-1) g v' u' g) - (tr(v g v g)+D*t^2)|
       <= 12(2R)^6 delta.
This reuses ComplexMatrixProductError.complex_matrix_list_prod_error and
EntryApprox.inv, followed by the existing real-to-complex trace map. No second
matrix product-error chain is defined.

If A^(-1)=A, u',v' belong to A^m, and g belongs to A^n, the witnessing word
belongs to A^(4m+2n). Both inverse membership and the exact word length are
proved. Thus m=2,n=4 costs16 factors; m=2,n=34 costs76 factors. Do not apply
the37-factor upper envelope to the latter without an additional argument.
The earlier arbitrary fixed-power envelope is available for the required length.

NATIVE COVERING-NUMBER CONSEQUENCE
For any indexed family u_i=U(s_i,t_i) with actual approximants z_i in A^m
satisfying the same uniform bounds, define independent-pair scalar values
  S={tr(u_j g u_j g)+D*t_i^2 : i,j}.
At any eta>0 with finite N_eta(A^(4m+2n)), the proved statement is
  N_eta(S) <= [24(2R)^6 delta/eta+5] N_eta(A^(4m+2n)),
including finiteness of N_eta(S). Native Frobenius covers, the existing trace
Lipschitz bound, and the existing one-dimensional patch refinement are reused.
In particular, the commuting approximation radius from R1822 must be inserted
as delta=12800 B0^6 eta/r0; the factor delta/eta is charged explicitly.
It cannot simply be replaced by a constant.

HOW THIS CHANGES THE PLAN
The scalar object is a concrete MIXED sum Y+D X, with X={t_i^2} and
Y={tr(u_j g u_j g)}. It is not yet the X+aX of the imported projection theorem.
The algebra explains a route through small mixed sums, additive Ruzsa bounds,
and the square-coordinate product identity, rather than assuming arbitrary
matrix multiplication becomes scalar addition. For Delta=0, the latter identity
loses its product term, so the generic division-by-Delta step would be invalid.
For very small Delta, inverse powers of Delta must be budgeted. The trace
separation formula itself has neither defect.

The comparison is currently to N_eta(A^(4m+2n)), not to |X| or N_eta(V).
Thus the cube-root normalization gap identified in R1825 remains real.
A lower bound for a cubic alternating product of the commuting family would
connect these scales and also permit a separate concentrated-cluster argument.
The source route treats concentration by a second branch; it does not license
inheriting normalized scalar bounds from those of the whole matrix set.
