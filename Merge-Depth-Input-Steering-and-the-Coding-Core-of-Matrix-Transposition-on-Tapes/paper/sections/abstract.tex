Can a deterministic multitape Turing machine transpose a $d\times d$ matrix with $b(d)=\max(1,\lceil\log_2d\rceil)$-bit
entries in time linear in the input length $L=d^2b(d)$? Assuming an affirmative answer to Question~9.1 of \emph{Directed
Recovery Structures in Tape Computations}, which asks whether storage codes on paired-distance separated relations of
bounded degree have rate at most one half, we locate where such a machine could still exist. For oblivious machines the
depth of the schedule no longer matters: a rank decomposition (hop charging) excludes every transposer whose code merges
deep data lineages only boundedly often along its paths, on every schedule, however deeply nested and however many cells
it revisits; for linear codes the linear crux of the series suffices. What remains for oblivious machines is depth
reduction of the code's merge structure. For adaptive machines we prove a form of an earlier low-adaptivity bound that
is robust against padding of bounded entry: if the work heads enter every cell boundedly often, the coarse skeleton of
the run, its head positions and output count rounded to multiples of a fixed width, must carry about $L/2$ bits. Hence
no linear-time transposer has bounded entry on every tape, and one whose work tapes have bounded entry must revisit
input cells unboundedly often and carry about half of the input in the coarse motion of its input head, a condition
that padding meets at no cost. Unconditionally, the source-cover exclusions for oblivious geometries extend to machines
of sufficiently low adaptivity, and every linear-time transposer, adaptive or not, yields an external-memory
transposition with linearly many I/Os that is oblivious on $2^{L-O(d)}$ inputs; an external-memory lower bound without
the indivisibility assumption would therefore refute linear-time transposition. We also prove a deep-nesting conjecture
of the series for pairs of block labels at one shift, conditional on a conjecture that straddle counts concentrate as
the shift varies, with partial results on that conjecture, and we rule out one natural building block for
constructions: Tanner codes assembled from projective planes, with every coordinate recovered from one line, violate the
paired-distance condition. Every numbered result is formalized in Lean~4 on the standard axioms; Question~9.1, the
linear crux and the straddle conjecture enter the formal statements as explicit hypotheses. The question itself remains
open.
