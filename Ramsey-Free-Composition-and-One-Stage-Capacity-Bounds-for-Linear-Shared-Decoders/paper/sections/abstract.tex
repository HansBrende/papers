We study two-stage compositions $K_2 \circ K_1$ and one-stage capacities of $F$-linear class-uniform
shared decoders on $\Mat_{d\times d}(F^b)$, following Section~6 of \cite{SharedDecoders2026} (which
used van der Waerden and Hales--Jewett arithmetic progressions to bound $d$ when $K_2 \circ K_1 = \tau$
with $M_1$ and $M_2$ column classes and to show that
$\rank_F(\tau - K_2 \circ K_1)/(bd) \to \infty$ as $d \to \infty$ at fixed $M_1, M_2$ when
$\operatorname{char}(F) \nmid d$, i.e.\ $(d : F) \ne 0$) and Problem~9.1(a)--(b) of
\cite{SeventeenthPaper2026} (one-stage linear shared-decoder capacity), together with three-term and
two-sided interval-mask swap codes (\cite[Remark~5.6 and Problem~9.3]{SeventeenthPaper2026}). No
multitape Turing machine is known to induce a one- or two-stage linear class-uniform decomposition;
working within these algebraic models, all numbered results are verified in Lean~4.

For two-stage linear composition, replacing arithmetic progressions by Ramsey-free additive structures
(avoiding van der Waerden and Hales--Jewett numbers) yields two bounds. First (\Cref{thm:2a}), over any
field $F$ and for $b, M_1, M_2 \ge 1$, replacing progressions by
affine Hilbert cubes extracted via iterated Cauchy--Schwarz proves that $K_2 \circ K_1 = \tau$ forces
\[
  d \;\le\; \max\bigl(8 M_1 M_2^2,\; (2 M_1)^{2^{M_2}}\bigr).
\]
For fixed $M_2 \ge 2$, this gives a cutoff polynomial in $M_1$---ruling out exact two-stage linear
factorizations whenever $M_1 = o\bigl(d^{1/2^{M_2}}\bigr)$ (including partitions of $\Z_d$ into
$M_1 = 2^{O(\sqrt{\log d})}$ $3$-AP-free classes obtained by translate-covering $\Z_d$ with a Behrend
set \cite{Behrend1946}) and whenever $M_2 \le (1-\eps)\log_2\log_2 d$ at fixed $M_1 \ge 2$ and
$\eps \in (0, 1)$---whereas at $M_2 = 1$, $M_1 = 1$, and small constant class counts such as
$M_1 = M_2 = 2$, Theorem~6.3 and Corollary~6.4 of \cite{SharedDecoders2026} already give the stronger
bounds $d \le M_1$, $d \le M_2^2$, and $d \le 16$. Second (\Cref{thm:2b}), over any field
$F$ with $\operatorname{char}(F) \nmid d$, combining shifted $(2M_2)$-fold monochromatic sumsets,
arbitrary-subset
Vandermonde pullbacks, sparse annihilating polynomials, and iterated K\H{o}v\'ari--S\'os--Tur\'an
extraction proves that for all $b, M_1, M_2 \ge 1$ and all $d \ge D_0(M_1, M_2)$,
\[
  \rank_F(\tau - K_2 \circ K_1)
  \;\ge\;
  c(M_1, M_2)\,b\,d\,(\log d)^{\frac{1}{2M_2 - 1}},
\]
giving $\Omega(b\,d\log d)$ when $M_2 = 1$ (\Cref{cor:2b-m2-one}) and an explicit log-power rate for
$M_1 \ge 2$ and large $d$ (at $M_1 = 1$, \cite[Theorem~6.6]{SharedDecoders2026} already gives
$\Omega(bd^2/M_2)$ for $d \ge 4M_2 + 1$, while here $D_0(1,1) = 16^{36} + 2 \approx 2.23 \times 10^{43}$).
Finite-dimensional controls at $d = 13$, $d = 4$, and $d = 12$ test the hypotheses of both bounds.

For one-stage linear shared decoders on \cite[Problem~9.1(a)]{SeventeenthPaper2026}, exploiting
decoder linearity on maximal monochromatic
cyclic runs proves an explicit single-shift intersection bound $B_{\mathrm{run}}(b,p,s)$ when
$\operatorname{char}(F) \nmid (d/\gcd(d,s))$ (\Cref{thm:1a-single-shift}, whose value
$\frac{1}{4}d^2 + \frac{3}{2}d$ on the alternating two-coloring for even $d = 2m \ge 2$ at
$b = 1, s = 1$ is realized over every field, \Cref{thm:1a-sharpness}). Averaging over shifts bounds
the maximum $b$-lane code dimension $D_b(p)$ by
$D_b(p) \le \frac{19}{32}\,bd^2 + \frac{3}{2}\,bd^{3/2}$ for two classes whenever
$\operatorname{char}(F) \nmid d$ (\Cref{thm:1b-two-class}, whose leading coefficient $\frac{19}{32}$
lies strictly below both the $\frac{41}{64}$ bound of \cite[Theorem~3.4]{SharedDecoders2026} and the
$\frac{5}{8} - o(1)$ nested-component certificate floor of \cite[Theorem~C.6]{SharedDecoders2026}),
and by
$D_b(p) \le \bigl(1 - \frac{5}{4M} + \frac{1}{M^2} - \frac{1}{4M^3}\bigr)bd^2 +
\bigl(\frac{5}{4} - \frac{1}{4M}\bigr)bd$ for $M \ge 2$ at primes $d \ne \operatorname{char}(F)$
(\Cref{thm:1b-multi-class}, giving $\frac{19}{32}\,bd^2 + \frac{9}{8}\,bd$ at $M = 2$; its leading
coefficient $c_M$ beats the flag bound $1 - \frac{1}{2M}$ for every $M \ge 2$ and is smaller than the
optimized difference-cover leading coefficient of \cite[Theorem~3.5]{SharedDecoders2026} if and only
if $2 \le M \le 40$, whereas \cite[Theorem~3.5]{SharedDecoders2026} has a $1 - \Theta(M^{-1/2})$
leading coefficient that is smaller for every $M \ge 41$ and applies to composite $d$ and arbitrary
characteristic). On \cite[Problem~9.1(b)]{SeventeenthPaper2026}, coupled $\Z_6$ Fourier-mode
elimination proves $\dim_F(\mathcal{X} \cap \rot_1^{-1}(\mathcal{X})) \le 14$ and hence
$D_1\bigl(\{0,1,3\},\{2,4,5\}\bigr) = 25$ over every field with
$\operatorname{char}(F) \notin \{2, 3\}$ (\Cref{thm:aperiodic-six-25}, leaving characteristics $2$
and $3$ open). For swap codes, we construct three-term families $\sum_{t=0}^2 A_t X B_t^{\T} = X^{\T}$
over every field exceeding $d(d+1)/2$ at $d \in \{6, 7, 9, 12\}$ and over every field at $d = 4$
(an example of \cite[Remark~5.6]{SeventeenthPaper2026}, previously checked over
$\F_2, \F_3, \F_5, \F_7$), and prove a characteristic-free two-sided bound for two-scale
(coarse--fine) families of \cite[Problem~9.3]{SeventeenthPaper2026}
(\Cref{thm:two-sided-coarse-fine,cor:two-sided-win}) giving, on contiguous fine singletons of
densities $\alpha = s_A/d, \beta = s_B/d \in (0,1)$ plus one coarse complement, leading coefficient
$\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4}\min\bigl(\beta(1+\alpha), \alpha(1+\beta)\bigr)$, strictly below the
one-sided Theorem~TP coefficient $\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2}\min(\alpha, \beta)$
(\cite[Theorem~6.4]{SeventeenthPaper2026}; and $\frac{11}{16}d^2 + d < \frac{3}{4}d^2$ at
$\alpha = \beta = 1/2$ for all even $d \ge 18$). Finally, we prove an Operator Schmidt Rank pass
lower bound ($t \ge \log_2 d$) for a restricted shuffle and row/column-uniform pipeline paired with
verified $O(L)$-time Turing machines ($L = d^2\max(1,\clog{d})$) in \texttt{lean-machine} for shuffle,
unshuffle, and within-row shear showing why $\OSR$ does not answer
\cite[Problem~9.5]{SeventeenthPaper2026}, together with cut-causal cone evaluation on frozen
\leanname{Transpose.TM} fibres, a Lean formalization of the column-class analogue of the
$1/(\Delta+1)$ random-order bound at entry read arity $\Delta$
(\cite[Proposition~4.14(a)--(b)]{FourthPaper2026}), and negative-bracket examples showing why two
specific column-class cut reductions fall short of \cite[Problem~9.4(b)]{SeventeenthPaper2026}.
Statement~(T) remains open.
