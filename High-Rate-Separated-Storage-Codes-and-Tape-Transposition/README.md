# High-Rate Separated Storage Codes and Tape Transposition

Hans Brende and GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI), November 2026.

[Read the paper](https://hansbrende.github.io/papers/High-Rate-Separated-Storage-Codes-and-Tape-Transposition/paper/main.pdf) · [Local PDF](paper/main.pdf) · [LaTeX sources](paper/) ([CC BY 4.0](paper/LICENSE)) · [Lean sources](lean/) ([Apache 2.0](lean/LICENSE))

## The question

If a deterministic multitape machine could transpose square matrices in linear time, what information would its tape computation have to preserve? Such a machine would force high-rate storage codes with widely separated input and recovery ports. We construct abstract codes meeting a stronger rate requirement, then identify what remains missing for a physical tape computation. Separation refers to distances in the helper incidence graph, not Hamming distance between codewords.

## What is proved

- **High-rate separated codes.** A fixed-degree linear seed of rate above one half over an odd-characteristic finite field, with diverging odd girth, supplies binary storage-code families of rate at least $1-C\Delta^{-\alpha}$ at every sufficiently large incidence-degree cap $\Delta$, where $C>0$ and $0<\alpha<1/2$ are fixed. At each fixed cap, length and minimum paired distance tend to infinity. A tensor majority operator gives the rank bound; binary syndrome fibres use the same coordinates. [Finite amplification](lean/TapeStorageRates/MajorityRank.lean) · [Support and separation](lean/TapeStorageRates/MajoritySupport.lean) · [Families](lean/TapeStorageRates/MajorityPartitions.lean)
- **One partition for every source subset and length.** At every sufficiently large length $L$, one encoder on all $L$ original coordinates has at most $2^{\varepsilon L+\sqrt L}$ attained labels, with $\varepsilon=C\Delta^{-\alpha}$. Its nonempty cells are storage codes. For every nonempty source subset $G$ and every real $u$, the uniform probability on $G$ of a cell rate below $\log_2|G|/L-\varepsilon-L^{-1/2}-u$ is at most $2^{-uL}$. Degree stays bounded by $\Delta$ and minimum paired distance diverges. These families rule out the unrestricted slow-gap premise of the packet exclusion criterion below. [All-length partitions](lean/TapeStorageRates/AllLengthConstruction.lean) · [Gap comparison](lean/TapeStorageRates/ConstructiveConsequences.lean)
- **Limits of simpler amplifiers.** Literal tensor checks create paired paths of length at most three on at least the product of the factor nullities. Actual local-projection deficits give an entropy ceiling, and a two-slot assignment with the stated load bounds forces rate at most $3/4$, without linearity. A sparse-rank inequality bounds the majority construction's exponent by $\alpha<1/2$. These are restrictions on the specified constructions, not on all storage codes. [Tensor checks](lean/TapeStorageRates/TensorObstruction.lean) · [Local-code ceiling](lean/TapeStorageRates/TannerCeiling.lean) · [Exponent](lean/TapeStorageRates/MajorityExponent.lean)
- **Packet transfer.** Fix a machine with $k$ work tapes. If dimension and entry width can grow together while the logarithmic rate of the timely successful inputs approaches one, every sufficiently large degree cap $\Delta$ admits binary storage codes with growing minimum paired distance and limiting lower rate at least $1-O(f_p(\Delta))$. Here $p=4k^2+1$ and $f_j(\Delta)=(\log_2\log_2\log_2\Delta/\log_2\log_2\Delta)^{1/j}$. Full success at arbitrarily large canonical dimensions suffices. A coding upper bound whose deficit exceeds this allowance by unbounded factors along a sequence of caps would force exponentially small success at all sufficiently large dimensions and widths. [Transfer and exclusion theorems](lean/TapeStorageRates/Main.lean)
- **Native bit positions.** The simulator computes the same partial function and makes its full motion traces inexpensive to specify. At each sufficiently large cap it works for any requested separation radius, all sufficiently large dimensions, and every positive entry width. Given a nonempty timely success set $G$, each cell of its partition projects to a code on at least $(1-2^{\sqrt{\log_2\Delta}}/\Delta)L$ physical bit positions. For $X$ uniform on $G$, the probability of a cell rate below $\log_2|G|/L-Af_p(\Delta)-u$ is at most $2^{-uL}$, on the same partition for every $u\ge0$. The packet and native bounds have the same exponent, by revealing whole batches and counting their joint record support. [Simulator](lean/TapeStorageRates/NativeCompiler.lean) · [Counting identity](lean/TapeStorageRates/NativeTypical.lean) · [Native rate theorem](lean/TapeStorageRates/NativeTypicalRates.lean)
- **Local prediction.** For separated targets, the sum of the information supplied by their permitted in-patch helpers is at most $2r$ times the total correlation of the input, where $r$ bounds overlap. If predictors score $m$ binary target occurrences with mean error $e$, then $m[1-h_2(e)]\le2r[n-H(X)]$. Noisy pairs attain equality. The distance test bounds the number of scored targets per patch, and exact coverage extends to finite alphabets of at least two symbols. [Information bound](lean/TapeStorageRates/PatchResidual.lean) · [Predictor errors](lean/TapeStorageRates/PatchPrediction.lean) · [Input-dependent choices](lean/TapeStorageRates/PatchPredictionEvent.lean)

All amplification, counting, padding, gap-comparison and barrier arguments, together with the tape-transfer and prediction theorems, are proved in Lean. For formalized results, the named declarations are authoritative and the manuscript gives human-readable proof outlines. The generated [axiom checks](lean/PaperAxioms.lean) cover every declaration named in the text or its formal status lines; their dependencies use at most `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

**Written seed proof.** The Lean construction takes [an explicit seed structure](lean/TapeStorageRates/OddGirthLinearSeed.lean) as a hypothesis, not an added axiom. Proposition 5.2 supplies it over $\mathbb F_{127^2}$ with rate $810/1613$ by applying [Paper 13](../Storage-Codes-above-Half-Rate-under-Paired-Distance-Separation/). That upstream arithmetic-geometric existence proof is not formalized here. Generic partition bounds, binomial tails, odd-power choices and padding indices reuse the shared [Lean library](../lean-machine/).

## What is open

Linear-time tape transposition remains open. The abstract construction rules out the unrestricted slow-gap route; a useful upper bound must exploit an additional restriction imposed by a physical tape computation. One proposed route would show that cells carrying a positive share of the inputs must admit useful local predictions at small overlap. On the constructive side, no efficient uniform tape algorithm is supplied for generating the coefficients, evaluating the encoder and recoveries, or producing the required output schedule. The all-length partition theorem is an existence result, not a tape implementation.

## Building and verifying

Clone the whole repository. The Lean package uses Papers 13 and 14, Paper 7's soft-recovery memo, and their sibling dependencies by relative path. Lean and Mathlib are pinned to v4.34.1. In a fresh checkout, prepare the transitive [Paper 2 metric dependency](../Local-Coding-Barriers-and-the-Limits-of-Tape-Geometry-for-Matrix-Transposition/lean/openai-metric/README.txt). From this paper's directory, run

```sh
(cd lean && lake exe cache get && lake build && lake env lean PaperAxioms.lean)
python3 tools/lean_index.py --check
(cd paper && latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex)
```

After changing declaration references or proof-status lines, run `python3 tools/lean_index.py` to regenerate both the index and the axiom checks.
