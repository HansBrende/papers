\section{What this settles, and how to screen variants}
\label{sec:scope}

The exact five implemented families in Paper~16's Section~10.3 are excluded
in the fixed-alphabet native interpretation of its cascade question
\cite[Problem~11.4]{SixteenthPaper2026}. They may pass the reported finite
crossing, separator and skeleton tests: those are necessary conditions,
and did not test the older all-regular-recursion obstruction. The proof
does not use experimental cut values or literal meanings assigned to
intermediate banks.

For a new variant, the reusable obligation is a charged stage decomposition
with regular actual fixed-stage profiles. Finite affine preparation and
cleanup, bounded-raster changes, or more teeth whose number is finite at
each fixed depth do not evade this test when the deep-stage time is still
uniformly summable. Nor does allowing arbitrary nonlinear rules or writes
at every positioning visit. One must verify the new complete trace; a name
such as ``cascade'' or a picture of its large-scale shape is insufficient.
No unlisted gallery variant or historical comparator is classified here.

A potential escape is a stage with a genuinely non-piecewise-affine limit
on positive physical time, or a failure of the fixed-stage or tail
hypotheses. Such an escape is only from this obstruction, not evidence
that the schedule can compute transpose. Likewise, growing work alphabets,
other width regimes and adaptive head or append schedules require separate
arguments. The imported theorem itself allows adaptive machines under
its quantified clock hypotheses; our five source applications and the
bit-lane bridge use fixed schedules.

For the impossibility program, this memo prevents further local-gate
searches on already excluded complete traces. For the construction program,
it identifies the relevant failure mode of these candidates: microscopic
irregularities and unbounded recursion depth still leave countably affine
macroscopic motion with no exceptional-time allowance. A new construction
must both escape that obstruction and perform the ordered computation.
The unrestricted question (T) remains open.
