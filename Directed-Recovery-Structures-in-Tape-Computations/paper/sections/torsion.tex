\section{A torsion diagnostic for finite-field recovery}\label{sec:torsion}

\begin{corollary}[Necessary torsion in bounded lifts]
\partformal{SmithRankLoss.lean}{smith_rank_loss,torsion_count_of_rank_and_nullity}{Existence of Smith normal form, the analytic rational-rank estimate and its asymptotic application, and the cokernel-torsion interpretation.}
\label{cor:dc-torsion}
Fix a prime $p$, integers $\Delta,t\ge1$, and $\varepsilon>0$.
Let $(E_n)$ satisfy the graph hypotheses of \cref{thm:dc-complex}.
Suppose matrices $M_n$ over $\mathbb F_p$ have diagonal blocks $I_t$,
off-diagonal support in $E_n$, and
$\dim\ker M_n\ge(1/2+\varepsilon)tN_n$.
For any integer lifts $\widetilde M_n$ with the same support restrictions,
literal diagonal blocks $I_t$, and uniformly bounded entries, at least
$(\varepsilon-o(1))tN_n$ of their \emph{nonzero} Smith invariant factors
are divisible by $p$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{informalproof}
Set $\rho_n=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}\widetilde M_n$.
\cref{thm:dc-complex} gives $\rho_n\ge(1/2-o(1))tN_n$.
The unimodular transformations in Smith normal form remain invertible
modulo $p$.  Thus
\[
 \#\{\hbox{nonzero invariant factors divisible by }p\}
 =\rho_n-\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_p}M_n
 \ge(\varepsilon-o(1))tN_n.
\]
Equivalently, the subgroup annihilated by $p$ in the integer cokernel
has at least this dimension over $\mathbb F_p$.  Zero invariant factors
give free abelian summands and are not counted as torsion.
\end{informalproof}

\textbf{Update (November 2026).} The thirteenth paper supplies a concrete
instance of \cref{cor:dc-torsion}~\cite[Theorems~A and~T, Lemma~5.2]{StorageCodes2026}.
Write $N_j$ for the number of code coordinates there. At all sufficiently
large levels, the displacement exceeds the fixed check radius, so no
off-diagonal check term descends to its own coordinate. The descended
checks therefore form a matrix with diagonal entries $1$ and off-diagonal
support in the stated recovery relation, and their kernel contains the
Tanner code. Restricting scalars from $\mathbb F_{127^2}$ to
$\mathbb F_{127}$ gives block width $2$, diagonal blocks $I_2$, and
kernel dimension at least $(8100/16130)\,2N_j$.
The incidence degree is fixed and the paired distances tend to infinity.
Thus, with $\varepsilon=8100/16130-1/2=7/3226$, every sequence of integer
lifts with the same support, literal diagonal blocks $I_2$, and uniformly
bounded entries has at least $(7/3226-o(1))\,2N_j$ nonzero Smith invariant
factors divisible by $127$. Such lifts exist by choosing bounded integer
representatives of the scalar entries. This is a written application of
the corollary; the thirteenth paper's arithmetic dimension bound and
construction steps are not fully formalized.

This torsion example refutes an unrestricted finite-field half-rate bound
under paired-distance separation. A fixed extension field is represented
over its prime field by enlarging the fixed block width. The analytic argument does not cover
growing block widths, unbounded complex coefficients, or nonlinear codes
on nonhyperfinite relations.  \cref{sec:mc-section} gives a separate
finite-field certificate on one existential expanding family, uniform
in the storage-code block width.  Neither result identifies all native
cone relations with that family or settles the native coding problem.
