PRESERVATION JUDGMENT
No additional wholesale analytic module retirement recommended. Modules with no
current executable consumers include useful independent affine return-graph,
full path small-cut, and native endpoint results. Removing them simply because
they are unused would hide mathematical scope. Several unused SL2 source-word
and covering specializations also contain distinct bounds, so no deletion was
proposed without a demonstrable replacement preserving that content.

These are bounded simplifications and one abstract affine hypothesis
strengthening. They do not improve the coefficient 1/2 in the omitted-time
bound, enlarge the native recursion exclusion, or settle unrestricted
transposition complexity. Root must Lean-check all proposals and integrate
successful changes into the source/axiom receipt before claiming completion.
