## The question

Section 6 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* ([`Capacity-and-Composition-of-Shared-Transposition-Decoders`](../Capacity-and-Composition-of-Shared-Transposition-Decoders/)) studied two-stage compositions `K₂ ∘ K₁` of `F`-linear class-uniform operators on `Mat_{d×d}(F^b)` with `M₁` and `M₂` column classes, using van der Waerden and Hales–Jewett arithmetic progressions to bound `d` when `K₂ ∘ K₁ = τ` and to show that `rank_F(τ - K₂ ∘ K₁) / (b d) → ∞` as `d → ∞` for fixed `(M₁, M₂)` when `char(F) ∤ d` (`(d : F) ≠ 0`). Can arithmetic progressions in that two-stage linear model be replaced by Ramsey-free additive structures—meaning structures extracted via Cauchy–Schwarz density cube bounds and Kővári–Sós–Turán extremal graph bounds without van der Waerden or Hales–Jewett progression numbers—with explicit bounds in `M₁`, `M₂`, and `d`, and what further dimension bounds follow in the one-stage linear shared-decoder and two-sided swap-code models of *Order, Symmetry and the Swap* ([`Order-Symmetry-and-the-Swap-A-Synthesis-of-Matrix-Transposition-on-Tapes`](../Order-Symmetry-and-the-Swap-A-Synthesis-of-Matrix-Transposition-on-Tapes/), Problems 9.1(a)–(b), Remark 5.6, and Problem 9.3) once decoder linearity and two-sided mask structure are used directly?

**Scope caveat.** No multitape Turing machine is known to induce a decomposition into one- or two-stage linear class-uniform stages. None of the results in this paper decides Statement (T) (`Transpose.LinearTimeTransposable`, linear-time multitape matrix transposition) or bridges general adaptive or oblivious Turing machines to linear shared decoders: (i) freezing genie advice or a trace cut on a finite-alphabet machine selects a *nonlinear subset* `C ⊆ Σ^{d×d}` rather than a linear subspace, and unbounded-window shared set decoders reach rate `1 - o(1)` (Theorem A.1 and Corollary A.2 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders*); (ii) at depth-`D` cone arity `Δ = b(M.k + 1)^D`, generic `O(1/Δ)` random-order deficits are exponentially smaller than cut-advice rate `ε = Ω(1/D)` (Propositions 4.13 and 4.14(c) of *Curved Motion, Depth Reduction, and Storage Codes for Matrix Transposition on Tapes*), while two naive column-class cut reductions—discarding cut-touching outputs, or positing a worst-case full-product class count across per-column cut slots (when `(M.q + 1)(M.g + 1)^{M.k} ≥ 2`)—give strictly negative brackets (`perColumnCutAbsorption_bracket_neg`), reinforcing the per-position fixed-alphabet formulation in the commentary on Problem 9.4(b) of *Order, Symmetry and the Swap*; and (iii) linear-time transposers must use curved, head-dependent, non-piecewise-affine motion on interacting work tapes (Theorems 3.10 and 3.12 and Corollary 3.24 of *Curved Motion, Depth Reduction, and Storage Codes for Matrix Transposition on Tapes*), while paired-distance-separated bounded-degree codes above half rate and approaching rate `1` ([`Storage-Codes-above-Half-Rate-under-Paired-Distance-Separation`](../Storage-Codes-above-Half-Rate-under-Paired-Distance-Separation/), [`High-Rate-Separated-Storage-Codes-and-Tape-Transposition`](../High-Rate-Separated-Storage-Codes-and-Tape-Transposition/)) rule out purely graph-theoretic bounded-degree closures.

## What is proved

Every numbered theorem, lemma, proposition, corollary, and example in the paper has a kernel-verified Lean 4 counterpart (`v4.34.1`, `27` public modules in `BoundedArityDecoders` plus `4` shared modules in `LeanMachine.ShuffleShear`, `117` status declarations and `229` total declarations audited in `PaperAxioms.lean`), depending at most on the standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` (with zero `sorry`, `admit`, `native_decide`, or custom axioms). Of the `41` numbered results, `38` are completely formalized (`\formal`) and `3` are marked `\partformal` with the following parts proved in the text only: the asymptotic `o(d^{1/2^{M₂}})` and `(1 - ε) log₂ log₂ d` specializations and the greedy translate-covering step from Behrend's `3`-AP-free set in Theorem 2A; the alternative `(b = 1, M₂ = 2)` parameter reading in Example 4.9; and the application of Theorem TP (Theorem 6.4 of *Order, Symmetry and the Swap*) to `InContiguousCoarseFineSpan` for the one-sided baseline in Corollary 7.5. Outside numbered theorem statements, remarks and prose comparisons with other papers (including the van der Waerden regime comparisons in Remarks 3.4 and 4.7(a), the finite crossover and `(u, v)`-minimization comparisons in Section 5 checked in exact rational arithmetic in `tools/verify_linear_capacity_crossovers.py`, the general `(m, s)` padded swap-code dimension formula in Section 7.1, the Mahler binomial remark in Remark 5.6 of this paper, and the numerical search in Remark 7.6) are proved in the text (or checked by script) only unless a Lean declaration is named.

1. **Exact two-stage linear composition via affine Hilbert cubes (Section 3, Theorem 2A).**
   - Over *any* field `F`, generalizing Lemma 6.1 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders*, if a subspace `W ≤ A` has an injective linear readout `L` invariant under the `2^k` ordered products `R_{k-1}^{ε_{k-1}} ⋯ R_0^{ε_0}` of `R_0, …, R_{k-1} ∈ End_F(A)`, then `(k + 1) dim_F W ≤ dim_F A + ∑_{t < k} dim_F ker(R_t - id)` (`lemma_AffineCubeCommonMessageGrowth`).
   - Extracting a monochromatic affine `M₂`-cube with steps in `{1, …, ⌊d / (2 M₂²)⌋}` by the classical Cauchy–Schwarz density cube lemma (`cauchySchwarzAffineCubeExtraction`) proves that for `b, M₁, M₂ ≥ 1`, `K₂ ∘ K₁ = τ` forces
     `d ≤ max(8 M₁ M₂², (2 M₁)^{2^{M₂}})` (`theorem2A_TwoStageRamseyFreeCutoff`, `theorem2A_GrowingClassesNonFactorization`).
     For fixed `M₂ ≥ 2`, this cutoff is polynomial in `M₁`—ruling out exact factorizations whenever `M₁ = o(d^{1/2^{M₂}})`, including partitions of `ℤ_d` into `M₁ = 2^{O(√(log d))}` `3`-AP-free classes obtained by translate-covering `ℤ_d` with a Behrend set (and whenever `M₂ ≤ (1 - ε) log₂ log₂ d` at fixed `M₁ ≥ 2` and `ε ∈ (0, 1)`)—whereas any progression-based cutoff from Theorem 6.3 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* is at least `M₂(W(M₁, 3) - 1)`, which is superpolynomial in `M₁`. At `M₂ = 1`, `M₁ = 1`, and small constant class counts, however, Theorem 6.3 and Corollary 6.4 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* are stronger: they give `d ≤ M₁`, `d ≤ M₂²`, and `d ≤ 16` at `M₁ = M₂ = 2`, where Theorem 2A gives `max(8M₁, 4M₁²)`, `max(8M₂², 2^{2^{M₂}})`, and `256`.
   - Finite controls: on the `3`-AP-free `2`-cube `J₁₃ = {0, 1, 4, 5} ⊆ ℤ₁₃` at `q = 2`, `9 ≤ max dim_F W ≤ 10` whenever `(13 : F) ≠ 0` (`control_D13TwoCubeAPFreeWitness`, `control_D13_GeneralFieldAndProgressionBridge`, exceeding the `≤ 8` progression bound on `4`-term APs); at `d = 4 = M₁ M₂` with `p₁ = p₂ = [0, 0, 1, 1]`, entry-copy and bit-lane-copy rules fail for all `b ≥ 1` over every alphabet `|Σ| ≥ 2`, while coded linear factorizations `K₂ ∘ K₁ = τ` exist for all lane widths `b ≥ 0` over every field with `(5 : F) ≠ 0` (`control_D4SameIntervalCodedFactorization`, `control_D4_AllWidthsAndBinaryFields`; characteristic `5` for `b ≥ 1` is open).

2. **Approximate two-stage linear composition via shifted sumsets (Section 4, Theorem 2B).**
   - Over any field `F` with `char(F) ∤ d` (`(d : F) ≠ 0`), combining minimum-shifted `(2 M₂)`-fold monochromatic sumsets (`unshiftedToShiftedSumset`, `unshiftedToShiftedSumset_explicit`), arbitrary-subset Vandermonde pullbacks on `SharedDecoders.bandRowSpace`, sparse annihilating polynomials, and iterated Kővári–Sós–Turán extraction proves that for all `b, M₁, M₂ ≥ 1` and all `d ≥ D₀(M₁, M₂)`, with natural logarithm `log = Real.log`,
     `rank_F(τ - K₂ ∘ K₁) ≥ c(M₁, M₂) · b · d · (log d)^{1 / (2 M₂ - 1)}`
     (`theorem2B_TwoStageLogPowerRankDeficit`, `theorem2B_ExplicitConstant`), and `Ω(b d log d)` when `M₂ = 1` (`theorem2B_OneSecondStage_OmegaLog`).
   - Explicit constants and comparison: `c(M₁, M₂) = 1 / (144 · (log(16 M₁ M₂))^{1/(2M₂ - 1)})` and `D₀(M₁, M₂) = max(4 M₂, (16 M₁ M₂)^{36^{2 M₂ - 1}} + 2)`. For `M₁ ≥ 2` and sufficiently large `d`, this replaces the iterated-logarithmic growth rates obtained from Theorem 6.6 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* via Gowers' van der Waerden bound by an explicit log-power rate. At `M₁ = 1`, Theorem 6.6 (`SharedDecoders.twoHop_rank_interval_any_field`) already gives `Ω(b d² / M₂)` for all `d ≥ 4 M₂ + 1`; at `M₁ = 2, M₂ = 1`, Theorem 6.6 at `k = 2` (`W(2, 5) = 178`, Stevens–Shantaram 1978) gives `b(d - 177)`, which Theorem 2B beats only for `d ≳ 10^{216}` (with formalized threshold `D₀(2, 1) = 32^{36} + 2 ≈ 1.53 × 10^{54}`, and `D₀(1, 1) = 16^{36} + 2 ≈ 2.23 × 10^{43}`).
   - At `d = 12, k = 2`, an explicit `24`-dimensional shift-covariant `4`-channel band witness (`q = 4`; formalized at `b = 2, M₂ = 1` where `b k d = 48` via `control_D12SingleClassAbstractBand`, `control_D12_BandRowSpaceChannelEquiv`) over `ℂ` on `J₁₂ = {0, 1, 3, 4, 7, 9}` shows under the text-only parameter reading `(b = 1, M₂ = 2)` (`24 = b k d`, so the band alone gives only `r ≥ 0`) that a single half-density band without any annihilating-polynomial step admits `b k d`-dimensional shift-covariant solutions, so in that reading the annihilating-polynomial steps cannot all be dropped.

3. **Run-extension bounds for one-stage linear shared decoders (Section 5; Problem 9.1(a) of *Order, Symmetry and the Swap*).**

Filtering by `rot_s`-eigenmodes over `F̄` proves that whenever `char(F) ∤ n` (`extension_Theorem1A_SingleShift`, `linearClassUniform_singleShift_finrank_le`),
     `dim_F(𝒳 ∩ rot_s⁻¹(𝒳)) ≤ B_run(b, p, s) := b g_s ∑_{a < g_s} ∑_{m < M} (T_{m,a}(n - d_{m,a}) - T_{m,a}²/2 + T_{m,a}/2) + b g_s d`
     (where `d_{m,a} = |J_m ∩ O_a|` and `T_{m,a} = |{c ∈ J_m ∩ O_a : c + s ∉ J_m}| ≤ n - d_{m,a}`). On the alternating two-coloring `p(c) = c mod 2` for even `d = 2m ≥ 2` at `b = 1, s = 1`, an explicit `2m(m + 1)`-dimensional linear class-uniform code over *every* field `F` (including `char(F) = 2`) realizes `dim_F(𝒳 ∩ rot_1⁻¹(𝒳)) = m² + 3m = d²/4 + 3d/2 = B_run(1, p, 1)`—attaining `B_run` with equality whenever `char(F) ∤ 2m`, and showing even when `char(F) ∣ 2m` that `m² + 3m` cannot be lowered—vs. `dim_F H_{{0, 1}} = d²` (`extension_Theorem1A_AlternatingSharpness`, `alternatingSharpCode_finrank_and_shift_inf`).
   - For two classes (`p : ℤ_d → Fin 2`, `d, b ≥ 1`), Cauchy–Schwarz across the `g_s` orbits gives `dim_F(𝒳 ∩ rot_s⁻¹(𝒳)) ≤ b(Q_s d / 2 - Q_s² / 4 + g_s Q_s / 2 + g_s d)` whenever `char(F) ∤ (d / g_s)` (`Q_s = |{c ∈ ℤ_d : p(c) ≠ p(c + s)}|`), and averaging across shifts for any `p : ℤ_d → κ` with `|κ| ≤ 2` (`d, b ≥ 1`) proves `D_b(p) ≤ (19/32) b d² + (3/2) b d^{3/2}` whenever `char(F) ∤ d` (`extension_Theorem1B_TwoClass`, `linearClassUniform_twoClass_finrank_le`), with leading coefficient `19/32` strictly below both the `41/64` bound of Theorem 3.4 and the `5/8 - o(1)` floor for the nested-component certificates of Theorem C.6 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* (whose isolation variant Theorem C.7 has floor `9/16 - o(1)`; neither is a lower bound on capacity, for any decoders); in prose / `tools/verify_linear_capacity_crossovers.py`, this bound is `< b d²` for `d ≥ 14` (beating Theorem 3.4 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* for all `d ≥ 1`), beats Theorem 3.5 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* for `d ≥ 35`, and is `< (3/4) b d²` for `d ≥ 93`. At prime `d ≠ char(F)` and `M ≥ 2` (`b ≥ 1`), shift averaging proves `D_b(p) ≤ c_M b d² + (5/4 - 1/(4M)) b d` with `c_M = 1 - 5/(4M) + 1/M² - 1/(4M³)` (`linearClassUniform_multiClass_prime_finrank_le_sharp`, `linearClassUniform_multiClass_prime_maxDim_le_sharp`, `linearClassUniform_twoClass_prime_finrank_le`, `extension_Theorem1B_MultiClassPrime`, `multiClassLeadingCoeff_sub_flag`), giving `(19/32) b d² + (9/8) b d` at `M = 2` (in prose / `tools/verify_linear_capacity_crossovers.py`: `< b d²` for primes `d ≥ 3`, beating Theorem 3.5 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* for primes `d ≥ 7`, and `< (3/4) b d²` for primes `d ≥ 11`). Its leading coefficient `c_M` beats the flag bound `1 - 1/(2M)` for every `M ≥ 2` (`(1 - 1/(2M)) - c_M = (M - 1)(3M - 1)/(4M³)`; whereas the difference-cover bound of Theorem 3.5 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* (`SharedDecoders.IsSharedCode.difference_cover_real_bound`) beats `1 - 1/(2M)` from `M ≥ 7` and its square-root form (`SharedDecoders.IsSharedCode.square_cover_bound`) from `M ≥ 11`) and, as verified in `tools/verify_linear_capacity_crossovers.py`, is strictly smaller than the optimized leading coefficient of `SharedDecoders.IsSharedCode.difference_cover_real_bound` **if and only if `2 ≤ M ≤ 40`** (improving on Theorem 3.5 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* at primes `d ≠ char(F)` past finite crossovers from `d ≥ 7` at `M = 2` up to `d ≥ 39,163` at `M = 40`). Conversely, `SharedDecoders.IsSharedCode.difference_cover_real_bound` (Theorem 3.5 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders*) has a `1 - Θ(M^{-1/2})` leading coefficient that is strictly smaller for every `M ≥ 41`, and applies to arbitrary composite `d` and arbitrary characteristic.

## Building and verifying

Clone the entire repository so relative Lake paths (`../../Capacity-and-Composition-of-Shared-Transposition-Decoders/lean`, `../../Order-Symmetry-and-the-Swap-A-Synthesis-of-Matrix-Transposition-on-Tapes/memo-class-uniform-codes/lean`, and `../../lean-machine`) resolve. cd ../lean-machine

```sh
lake build
lake env lean Axioms.lean
```

```sh
cd ../Ramsey-Free-Composition-and-One-Stage-Capacity-Bounds-for-Linear-Shared-Decoders

cd lean
lake exe cache get
lake build
lake env lean PaperAxioms.lean

python3 tools/verify_linear_capacity_crossovers.py

```
