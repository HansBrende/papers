We study cover structure and local generation as restrictions on linear
storage codes. For the prescribed binary adjacency check on Clebsch,
every connected binary-lift tower has kernel dimension at most $N/2+2$.
Either equality and signing rigidity hold, or dimension is at most half
and remains so at every descendant. The constant two is sharp at every
level. The bound extends to regular deck groups $P\times D$ and
$P\times S_3$, where $P$ is a finite $2$-group and $D$ is odd abelian;
it is sharp at every cyclic degree. Reciprocal width-$r$ Clebsch checks
have rank at least $6r$, equality exactly for flat gains, and no rank
$6r+1$. A width-five counterexample rules out a stronger general bound.

For homogeneous checks on a fixed free-group voltage template, under
specified ambient pro-$p$ and separating-chain hypotheses, disconnected
matched ports in the infinite incidence cover imply asymptotic half
rate at fixed width, uniformly over coefficient fields and values.
For growing widths, excess rate requires positive dimension in simple
coefficient factors exceeding every fixed size. Independent-chord
covers with a spanning $2$-factor have uniformly vanishing rate for
nonzero scalar checks and for invertible blocks preserving a common
full flag. Exact homogenization preserves rate but can create one
large simple factor. Ordinary group-word recovery inherits the scalar
cover bounds through its exponent check.

Separately, in characteristic two and with odd girth greater than
$2R+1$, the code dimension plus the dimension spanned by radius-$R$
codewords is at most the sum of attained coordinate dimensions.
A nonlinear encoder with recoverable affine hull consequently pays
for excess support size or entropy through its nonlocal sources.
Exact homogenization and the dimension interfaces are Lean-verified.
The finite and asymptotic cover theorems and the graph-local and
source/group-word applications have complete written proofs but are
not formalized. No general nonlinear bound or native tape realization
is supplied.
