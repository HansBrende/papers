\section{Formalization boundary and verification}\label{sec:formalization}

The library imports the earlier native-relations package with the repository's
pinned Lean/Mathlib toolchain. It reuses the frozen \texttt{Transpose.TM} model,
the published batch simulator and the shared \texttt{lean-machine} library.
No frozen statement is changed. Each numbered result names its checked
endpoint or states the remaining gap; the generated index lists the declarations.

\paragraph{Actual native replay and counting.}
The adaptive replay proof starts from actual native execution prefixes.
Chronological write dictionaries recover work reads from their true latest
parents. Comparing two runs with the same paid record recovers their states,
positions, old tuples and output ordinals, then proves input injectivity.
This comparison needs no total decoder on invalid messages. The finite
metadata and sparse support/value menus are counted separately. Neither a
common fine trace nor a suitable selected chunk is assumed.

The return theorem derives its slab from each timely correct run, retains
the snapshot-adjusted geometric margin, and proves its uniform large-dimension
tail for all positive entry widths. The input-cut theorem uses literal
endpoint-inclusive separators in the original dependency graph. It constructs
the cut-free ancestors, derives their state and latest-parent closure, and
recovers the input from the actual fragment record. Its sparse-menu estimate
handles every cut budget without a half-runtime restriction. The localized
variant derives the snapshot overlays and exterior-parent coverage from each
actual selected slab. Its final off-carrier expansion bound controls every
valid slab and every free head, with targets in the complete original run.
The dimension thresholds precede the width, machine and input quantifiers.
The coherent-cluster extension proves multi-core replay and constructs its
finite metadata from actual signed charts. Only the rounded endpoints are
counted, not descriptions of real functions. Its return and cut tails hold
simultaneously for all clusters, signs and charts; the cut statement covers
every valid selected slab and all smaller excursion thresholds.

The getter replay includes actual observer updates, continuous port positions,
the shared label, strict prior output and joint sparse corrections. Bounded
metadata is extracted from actual runs. The paid scan loader separately
implements its transition table on the frozen native model: it takes exactly
$2P+4$ steps, has the stated finite state and alphabet cardinalities, and
continues the arbitrary consumer without a linking step. For a consumer that
preserves each archive symbol it reads, the reached-configuration invariant
and shared scan checkpoints construct total local getters with zero errors
throughout preparation and consumption. The resulting success bound is
formalized, including the case of no scan archives.

\paragraph{The complete block-program lemma.}
The producer is an actual total causal program. One shifted partition pays
both its switch states and distinct foreign versions. Chronological replay
proves full-cube product transcript fibres and group-local recovery; the
entropy proof applies to one arbitrary input law, without independence.
The consumer is one complete read-only operation word. Its window entry
states and output records are obtained by slicing that execution, and its
window read counts sum to at most the actual global count. Thus
\cref{lem:feedback-product}, including the constant $72$, is formalized.
The theorem does not itself compile the native tape monitor or output spool.

\paragraph{Reusable finite components.}
The shared library supplies monotone sample and signed-position menus,
finite-field hash separation, common context pruning, actual top sets,
weighted selection, strict small-fibre bounds, time grids and bounded-degree
tape-word graph balls. The persistence selection uses these actual top sets
under the original law to choose one tape set, one live interval and one
retained event. Its conditional bounds concern the whole native tape words:
blank extension identifies the finite horizon word with the infinite word.
Native correctness bounds the actual strict-prefix fibre and hence the number
of bits still undelivered. The uniform-source wrappers derive their half-mass escape events from the
literal native thin-source count, including its factor two where required.
Every subsequent selection, probability and uniform rate estimate is checked.
For remote persistence, one common finite observation window is used while
each input retains its own first halt time; no frozen post-halt configuration
counts as a future read. The native catalogue theorems remain explicit
premises. The numerical feedback tails and finite weighted-date conclusions
are likewise checked from the stated native monitor inequalities.
The carrier/reference/cache corollaries derive their complete state and label
budgets from the literal finite products, including ceilings, default states
and every syndrome. Their common native simulation contracts remain explicit.
The reference and cache tails retain the common-hash factor two. Actual
unchanged-cell intervals and changed-address duration/span bounds are also
checked, including episodes truncated at the successful halt horizon.

Other checked components include the continuous order-conflict chart,
protected cores and paid fringes, actual changed-cell supports, shared scan
checkpoints, joint entropy invoices and input erosion. The frozen-trace cut
invoice derives input recovery from the native field recursion and the literal
separator; its field-only form pays an output bit only at cut desired roots.
This information step does not establish a geometric simulator lift.

The actual aligned simulator's batch clock, terminal drain, head shadows and
FIFO bounds are checked. The guarded-path theorem constructs and counts
literal simulator vertices, and projects surviving paths through actual source
state and last-visit edges. It includes both path endpoints and needs no
shared source trace. Raw-cone transport is also checked, including the
version with no terminal-output charge when the future ordinal exists.
The finite eroded-input invoice combines an actual lifted cut with the full
native frozen-trace injection. The entry-capacity theorem extracts an actual
nonlinear storage code from a joint guard/exterior fibre; its source relation
retains all lanes and self-edges. One common simulator-trace event controls
every original capacity certificate, with no union over their parameters.
Crossing-target transport is also checked: literal epoch labels bound the
discarded targets, actual source crossings supply the surviving connectors,
and long-hop packing pays the target guards. The finite source-cut lift is
combined with the correct-subset crossing theorem of the earlier paper and
uniform budgets to derive expansion on the original executions.

\paragraph{Remaining execution-level obligations.}
The dynamic-carrier theorem requires a new transition-table compiler, a
physical visited-interval invariant through arbitrary handoffs, partial
sparse decoding and an exact state count. The existing batch simulator is a
different machine. The catalogue and raw-relative handoffs, historical-cache
policy, last-visit eraser and redundant-archive wrapper also need their stated
native constructions. Consequences of those constructions retain the same
explicit dependency.

The whole-run feedback theorem requires a native
block monitor and paid output spool through actual halt, with the complete
state and event budgets and preservation of the dated-version statistic.
The native constructions remain formalization todos. The simulator transport
adapters are complete over the existing aligned machine. A finite helper is
never substituted for a missing execution
correspondence.

Every cited Lean declaration must use only \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}, as checked by the generated
axiom audit. No proof is admitted, and no additional axiom or kernel-bypassing
computation is used. The English outlines of formalized results are reading
aids; their Lean statements and proofs are authoritative. This paper is not
a fully formalized proof of every native theorem it contains.
