\section{Formalization and reproduction}
\label{sec:verification}
The reduction and compiler barriers have Lean proofs. For those results,
the Lean proofs are the mathematical proofs; the human-readable proofs and
outlines explain them without enlarging their checked statements. The map
in \lean{verification/contracts.json} identifies these results and their
supporting native interfaces.

\Cref{thm:paid-phase,lem:phase-marker,thm:normalizer-word} instead have
complete written proofs in \cref{sec:phase}, including the native tape
schedules. They are explicitly marked \emph{not formalized} and are listed
separately in \lean{verification/written-results.json}. The manuscript
checker requires every numbered result to occur in exactly one of these
two proof-status categories. A successful Lean audit does not certify the
written results. Formalization remains to cover the vector-polynomial
recurrence and selector, the all-size phase schedule, the marker lemma,
and both native normalizer reductions.

The new package shares the native machine model and supporting results of
\cite{FirstPaper2026,SecondPaper2026} through a relative Lake dependency.
It preserves the existing Lean and mathlib pins
\cite{Lean4,Mathlib2020,Mathlib4}. Shared definitions are imported, not copied
and independently redefined. The formalized native execution claims are proved
against actual transition traces, including input preparation and final
output. The new phase and normalizer schedules are written implementation
proofs; they have not been compiled into checked transition traces.

The reproduction script builds the mapped modules, checks their aggregate
imports, and audits every declaration owned by their local import closure.
It records source hashes, elaborated endpoint types and the axiom dependencies.
The permitted foundations are \lean{propext}, \lean{Classical.choice}, and
\lean{Quot.sound}. The checks reject additional axioms, admitted proofs and
unchecked native decision procedures. These checks complement the review of
the mathematical contracts and the English-to-Lean correspondence; compilation
alone does not establish that correspondence.

The paper's README gives the build and verification commands. Clone the whole
repository so the sibling Lean packages retain their relative paths. Generated
local logs are ignored; the sources and reproduction tools contain no
machine-specific paths. The manuscript build is separate from the Lean audit.
