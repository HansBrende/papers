NEW STRENGTHENINGS IN THIS AUDIT

Let P=n^2*b, D=P-log2|F| and H(Z) be actual helper Shannon entropy in bits
for uniform input on nonempty F.

A. Two-stage entropy toll:
  D + H(Z) >= b*r*(n-r), for every0<=r<=n;
  D + H(Z) >= b*floor(n^2/4).
The proof uses the already-proved cardinal bound for each branch and existing
mean_log_fiberCard. The finite attained-helper subtype allows an infinite
ambient helper type. Since H(Z)<=log2|image Z|<=g, this is stronger than the
old g-bit conclusion when helper frequencies are unequal. A concrete correct
example illustrating strict numerical entropy-vs-range difference is identity
on all8 symmetric Boolean2x2 matrices, with helper X00 AND X11: helper
frequencies3/4 and1/4 give about0.811 bits versus a one-bit range bound.
Lean: TwoStageHelperEntropy.helper_entropy_deficit_bound and
quarter_helper_entropy_deficit_bound.

B. Path/cycle XOR finite constant:
  B >= P - 3*b*n*sqrt(n), replacing4*b*n*sqrt(n).
This retains all cycle-embedding/path/subcycle hypotheses. The natural bound
is P<=B+3*b*n*floor(sqrt(n)). Existing ball estimates suffice; no new asymptotic
claim is made. Lean: XorPathCycleBound's existing endpoints.

C. Adaptive finite treewidth improvement:
  E[tw] >= (n/4)*alpha^2 + alpha/2 - 1,
  alpha=max(epsilon-2(D+H(Z))/P,0).
The floor-threshold proof had discarded the nonnegative product
(r-floor(r))*(floor(r)+1-r). Retaining it gives the additional alpha/2 term.
Lean: TreewidthAverage.quadratic_bound_of_integer_tests and
AdaptiveTreewidth.average_treewidth_lower_bound.
