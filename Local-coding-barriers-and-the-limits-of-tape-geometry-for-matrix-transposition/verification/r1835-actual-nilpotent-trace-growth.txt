1. EXACT SHIFTED TRACE PRODUCT, WITH ITS GENERAL CORRECTION RETAINED.
For any real2x2 b with tr(b)=0, any real2x2 g, and real c,t,p,q, write U=cI+t b,V=pI+q b, ell=tr(bg),tau=tr(g),Delta=det(b). Then
  tr(U g V g)=(c*tau+t*ell)(p*tau+q*ell)-2(cp+Delta*t*q)det(g).
This polynomial identity does NOT assume det(b)=0 or det(g)=1.
For u,v,g in SL2(R), det(b)=0, c,p>=1/2 and the above forms, the already proved determinant-one coordinate equation forces c=p=1. Consequently
  tr(u v g)+tau=(tau+ell*t)+(tau+ell*q),
  tr(u g v g)+2=(tau+ell*t)(tau+ell*q).
These are the shifted identities needed for the SAME scalar input set, including its positive offset. They are standard trace algebra, not a claim of novelty.
