1. INDEXED ROW ENERGY AND POPULAR OVERLAPS.
For finite index set I, finite universe P, and rows R_i subset P, including repeated rows,
  (sum_i |R_i|)^2 <= |P| * sum_i sum_j |R_i intersect R_j|.
This composes mathlib Cauchy--Schwarz with finite indicator double counting. Retaining indices is essential; replacing the row family by a set of distinct rows would lose multiplicity.
If I is nonempty, K>0, every row has |I| elements and |P|<=K|I|, there exist i in I and E subset I such that
  |I| <= 2K|E|,
  |I| <= 2K|R_i intersect R_j| for every j in E.
The bounds are stated without division, so there is no unrecorded integer rounding loss.

2. TWO IMPORTED RUZSA INEQUALITIES.
For finite A,B in any additive commutative group,
  |A+B| * |A intersect B| * |A| <= |A+A|^2 * |B+B|.
For A nonempty and K>0, if both |A+A| and |B+B| are at most L|A| and |A|<=2K|A intersect B|, then
  |A+B| <= 2K L^3 |A|.
No independent sign assumption on L is needed: the meaningful size hypotheses already constrain it. This is a short composition of mathlib's sub/add/add and add/sub/add Ruzsa inequalities, not a reproof of Ruzsa.
