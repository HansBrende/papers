\section{Proof status and verification}\label{sec:verification}

Every numbered result carries an explicit status line. Thirteen new numbered
results are fully Lean-verified: conditioning, fibres, independent coordinates,
rectangles, threshold pullback, the general row-word deficit, port colors, separated components, finite
separators, hyperfiniteness, damaged expanders, the rank identity, and the
matrix-label certificate.

Six results are partially formalized. The paired-distance and self-demand
bounds use native transport estimates that remain written proofs. Degree
trimming has formal finite counting and degree bounds, but its native premise
and asymptotic application are unformalized. The finite and asymptotic
code deficit is formalized from an actual row-word cover; the native extraction
and its uniform sublinear exception bound are unformalized. The expanding-family
linear-code corollary is formalized from actual matrix labels, including
linearization of nonlinear recovery; constructing the family and its witnesses
is unformalized. The bounded-lift torsion count is formalized from a supplied
unimodular diagonalization and rank/nullity estimates; Smith-form existence,
the analytic estimate, and the cokernel interpretation remain written proofs.

The other twenty-three new numbered results have written proofs. These include
the cyclic-component bound, its native cleanup obstruction, and the
central-layer group transfer with its exceptional-row corollary. Two further
numbered results are cited from earlier papers at a pinned revision, with their
verification scope stated explicitly. The general all-code paired-distance
bound, unrestricted cheap depth reduction, and an all-input treatment of
adaptive schedules remain open.

The Lean library uses Lean and Mathlib v4.34.1 and requires the shared
\texttt{lean-machine} package and the fourth paper's library from the same repository. No new axioms,
\texttt{sorry}, \texttt{admit}, or kernel-bypassing decision procedures are permitted.
The only permitted axioms are \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and
\texttt{Quot.sound}. The declaration index and \texttt{PaperAxioms.lean} are generated
from the same inline formal-status annotations by \texttt{tools/lean\_index.py}.

From the paper directory, run \texttt{python3 tools/lean\_index.py} to regenerate
these files. In \texttt{lean/}, run \texttt{lake exe cache get}, \texttt{lake build},
and \texttt{lake env lean PaperAxioms.lean}. The entire repository must be present
with its sibling directories. In \texttt{paper/}, run
\texttt{latexmk -pdf main.tex} to compile the manuscript.

Formal verification, where recorded, establishes the precise Lean statements;
it does not certify external novelty or remove hypotheses from their applications.
