2. EXACT FIXED-g TRACE ARITHMETIC.
Let tr(b)=0, u=cI+t b, v=pI+q b be in SL2(R), and let g be in SL2(R). Write
  ell=tr(bg), tau=tr(g), Delta=det(b).
Then
  tr(u v g) = (cp-Delta*t*q)*tau + (c*q+t*p)*ell,
  tr(u g v g^-1) = 2cp+t*q*((2-tau^2)*Delta-ell^2).
The second formula is proved first as a polynomial identity retaining det(g), then specialized using the existing determinant-of-commutator trace polynomial. No diagonalization is used.
If Delta=0 and c,p>=1/2, the existing determinant-one coordinate equation implies c=p=1, and the formulas become
  tr(u v g) = tau+ell*(t+q),
  tr(u g v g^-1) = 2-ell^2*(t*q).
Thus the SAME quantitatively nonzero ell reads addition and multiplication. The coefficient lambda=-1 in R1831 was specific to its inverse-final projection expression; it does not remove these ring operations. These algebraic identities are supporting tools, not novel impossibility or expansion theorems.
