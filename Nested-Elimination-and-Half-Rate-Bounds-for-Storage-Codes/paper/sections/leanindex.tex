\texttt{HalfRate}. The declarations named in status lines are grouped by the module named there; those named only
in the text are listed at the end. All of them depend only on the axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and
\texttt{Quot.sound} (\cref{app:verify}). The declarations of the earlier libraries of the series that are named in
the text, in the namespaces \texttt{Transpose} of the first paper, \texttt{MotionDepthCodes} of the fourth paper,
\texttt{CircularMemo} of its circular memo and \texttt{DirectedRecovery} of \emph{Directed Recovery Structures},
are defined and verified there~\cite{FirstPaper2026,FourthPaper2026,CircularMemo2026,DirectedRecovery2026}.
