\section{Replay without a common trace}\label{sec:replay}

Fix public integers $T_*\ge P$, $W\ge1$ and $H\ge0$. On a timely correct
input choose a free head $f$, a slab $S=[\ell,\ell+W)\cap\Z$, and an outer
interval $[a_0,b_0)\subseteq[0,T)$. Retain
\[
 C=\{t\in[a_0,b_0):y_f(t)\in S\},
\]
whose nonempty maximal runs are $[a_i,e_i)$, $1\le i\le r$. The choices may
depend on the input.

The geometry message records $f,\ell,a_0,b_0,r$ and, for each run, its time
endpoints, all entry head positions, the raw-head minimum and maximum, and
$o(a_i),o(e_i)$. Unit motion implies that the raw positions visited in a run
are exactly the integer interval between its extrema. Consequently the message
determines the raw coordinate set $I$ read in $C$, the output ordinals $O$
appended in $C$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:omitted}
 U=\pi(O)\setminus I.
\end{equation}
The detailed paths and append flags inside the runs are not recorded.

\begin{lemma}[Incoming first reads]\label{lem:first-read}
\formal{IncomingReturns.lean}{outside_first_read_is_long_return}
Suppose $t$ is the first visit during $[a,e)$ to a cell outside the radius-$H$
window about $y_h(a)$. If the cell has a previous global visit $p$, its last
such visit satisfies $p<a<t$, and $(p,t)$ is a return with excursion greater
than $H$, witnessed by the entry time $a$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{outline}
A first run-visit cannot have its last predecessor inside the run. The entry
time itself cannot be the read because it lies at the window center. These
strict inequalities produce a genuine nonadjacent return; no $H+1$ loss is
needed.
\end{outline}

For every nonfree head, tag precisely the first reads in each run that are
outside the entry window and have a previous global visit. Attach their old
symbols to their typed slots $(t,h)$. There are at most as many tags as actual
off-carrier long returns, by \cref{lem:first-read}; the actual typed-slot
injection is checked as \lean{incoming_support_le_returns}. If at most $J$ tags are
used, one joint event/value menu has size
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sparse}
 \mathcal S(n,J,A)=\sum_{j=0}^{\min(J,n)}\binom nj A^j,
 \qquad n=(k-1)T_*,\quad A=|\Gamma|.
\end{equation}
One may enlarge $n$ to $kT_*$. Repeated consumers of different versions are
different events. There is no independent $J\log P$ address payment.

\begin{lemma}[Joint sparse records]\label{lem:sparse-menu}
\formal{RecordCounts.lean}{sizedImports_card,sparseImports_card,replay_message_bound}
The finite type choosing at most $J$ of $n$ typed slots and one of $A$ values
for each chosen slot has the cardinality in \cref{eq:sparse}. An injective
encoding into this type, $M$ metadata choices, $e$ exterior bits and $p$ payload
bits bounds its input set by $M2^{e+p}\mathcal S(n,J,A)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{outline}
For exactly $j$ slots choose their subset and list the values in increasing
slot order. The respective cardinalities are $\binom nj$ and $A^j$. Sum over
$j$; terms with $j>n$ vanish. The second claim is the cardinality inequality
for an injection into the explicit product type. Construction of the native
encoding is proved next from the actual successful executions.
\end{outline}

\begin{theorem}[Finite adaptive replay]\label{thm:replay}
\formal{ReplayGeometry.lean}{native_fixed_geometry_replay_bound,integerSlab_card}
For fixed geometry with $r$ runs, let $\iota=1$ if $a_0>0$ and $0$ otherwise.
The number of timely correct inputs represented by that geometry and at most
$J$ tagged imports is at most
\begin{equation}\label{eq:replay-bound}
 2^{P-|U|}Q^r A^{\iota W+(k-1)r(2H+1)}\mathcal S((k-1)T_*,J,A).
\end{equation}
This statement permits different original traces within the same message class.
\end{theorem}
\begin{outline}
The encoder records the input outside $U$, each run-entry state, the nonfree
radius-$H$ entry windows, the free slab at $a_0>0$, and the actual joint
typed import record. A tagged blank is a present value, distinct from an
absent tag. At $a_0=0$ the slab record has an empty domain.

Compare two actual successful executions with equal records. A simultaneous
induction proves equality of their retained control and instructions, and
of every free-slab cell throughout the outer interval. An omitted step
writes outside the slab in both executions. At a retained step, a nonfree
read agrees by its last in-run write, its paid entry window, or its equal
incoming tag. For a first outside-window read with no tag, the actual cell
is virgin and blank. The raw symbols agree by the exterior record and the
recorded extrema, with public endmarkers outside the input. Equality of the
complete old tuple determines the simultaneous native transition. These
are the checked declarations
\lean{ExecutionPrefix.adaptive_replay_agreement} and
\lean{ExecutionPrefix.replay_output_count_agreement}.

The maximal-run decomposition supplies the entry map and every retained
prefix. The native output counter increases by zero or one, so every ordinal
between a run's recorded entry and exit counters has an actual append in
that run. This derives output coverage from the geometry itself. Equal
instructions and entry counters then give equal retained append ordinals and
bits. Actual final-output correctness recovers $U$; the exterior gives the
other coordinates. The resulting \lean{encodeReplay_injective} compares
actual runs directly and assumes no common detailed trace or decoder.

The state, window and slab products have exactly the displayed sizes.
\lean{replayImports_card_le} bounds the actual Option-valued sparse tuple
by the joint support/value menu, including its event positions. Terms above
the number of typed slots vanish. The formal theorem allows any finite free
cell set and any demand map $\pi$; a width-$W$ integer slab has exactly $W$
cells and gives \cref{eq:replay-bound}.
\end{outline}

If $r\le N$, the following safe metadata menu will be used:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:metadata}
 \mathcal M=k(N+1)(2T_*+W+2)(T_*+1)^2
 \bigl[(T_*+1)^4(2T_*+1)^{k+3}\bigr]^N.
\end{equation}
A nonempty slab has $-T_*-W+1\le\ell\le T_*$. Every head coordinate and
raw extremum lies in $[-T_*,T_*]$; every time and output counter is in
$[0,T_*]$. There are two outer endpoints, and per run two time endpoints,
two output counters, $k+1$ entry positions and two extrema. Padding to $N$
runs gives \cref{eq:metadata}. The actual bounded encoder and exact
cardinality are checked as \lean{SlabObservation.encode_times} and
\lean{replayMetadata_card}. All correlations only reduce this product
bound. At fixed $k,N$ and $T_*,W=O(P)$ its logarithm is $O(\log(P+1))$.
