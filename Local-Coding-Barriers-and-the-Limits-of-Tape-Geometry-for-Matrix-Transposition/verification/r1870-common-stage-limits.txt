   actual stage interiors, not just an abstract measurable superset.

   subsequence for all node volumes, stage endpoints and the halt time.

   V_i(w)>=0; 0<=rho<1; a>=0; child-volume sum<=rho*parent volume;

   of T/P, A/P, E/P and V/P for every fixed key, 0<=l_p<=r_p<=H,

   budget and endpoint/volume convergence. Thus an ordered limiting stage
   with v=0 collapses. Positive limiting duration requires positive v.
6. zero_volume_call_duration_tendsto proves (D_i-S_i)/P_i -> 0 for a
   contiguous call whose tick membership is exactly its subtree prefix,
