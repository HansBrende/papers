Scope

  nonlinear banks with forward column locality and interval recovery.
  Exact finite helper bound, arbitrary input laws, reachable-state budgets,
  P-o(P) consequence, and explicit O(P/log log d) loss.

  Expander-shear every-half recovery obstruction, including correlated laws,
  randomized helper selection, independent raw/stored row orders and separate
  within-row bijections. Average branch budgets are charged against the exact
  conditional source deficit P-H(X|Z). The companion fixed native encoder is assembled
  from paid rational metadata, row clocks, matching scans and raw output.
