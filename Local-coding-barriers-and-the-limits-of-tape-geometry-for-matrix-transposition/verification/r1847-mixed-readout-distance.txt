MAIN METRIC THEOREM: mixed_readout_distance_bound
Let b be traceless, M>=1, rho>0, ||b||,|det b|<=M, and
 ||Ad_g(b)||<=M. Let u=sI+t b and v=pI+q b be determinant-one
matrices with dist(u,I),dist(v,I)<=1/8. Write
 Delta=det b, D=det(bg-gb), W=v u^-1 g(vu)g^-1.
If |D|>=rho and |D-4Delta|>=rho, then
 max(dist(u,I),dist(v,I)) <= (144 M^2/rho) dist(W,I).
The conclusion bounds BOTH inputs. Either input may equal I. No
nonzero-determinant assumption on b is needed for this metric statement.

Proof: a=vu,w=uv^-1,E=Ad_g(a)-w=w(W-I). Commutativity gives
u^2=aw,v^2=aw^-1. The earlier identity gives
|det([b,Ad_g b])|=|D||4Delta-D|>=rho^2.
Instead of a 4x4 frame inverse, the norm bound gives
||[b,Ad_g b]||>=rho/2. Commuting E with b and Ad_g b bounds the
two noncentral coefficients by4M||E||/rho. The chart bounds a,w
by12M^2||E||/rho; their norms and the inverse norm are<=3.
Squaring costs4 and E costs3, yielding144M^2/rho.
