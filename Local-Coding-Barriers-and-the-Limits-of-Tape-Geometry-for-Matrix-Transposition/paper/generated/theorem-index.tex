\paragraph{Dependency structure.}
The original model and $M^*$ theorem feed the native motion and approximation
obstructions, which feed the scan-palette and recursion exclusions. The
finite original-row, two-coordinate packet and two-stage interfaces support the
three local-coding families independently of the analytic padding chain.
For padding, rational expansion feeds finite overlap graphs and their
paid excursion implementation; cleanup and payload composition preserve
the computation, and the original cut-to-limit theorem supplies the
full $M^*$ conclusion. These are logical dependencies; the manuscript
order is chosen for exposition rather than to reproduce the import graph.
