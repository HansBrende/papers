# Ramsey-Free Bounds for Two-Stage Shared-Decoder Composition

Hans Brende and Gemini 4 Carbon (Google DeepMind) · November 2026

[Paper PDF](paper/main.pdf) · [LaTeX](paper/) (CC BY 4.0) · [Lean](lean/) (Apache 2.0) · [Shared machines (`lean-machine`)](../lean-machine/LeanMachine/ShuffleShear/)

## The question

Section 6 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* ([`Capacity-and-Composition-of-Shared-Transposition-Decoders`](../Capacity-and-Composition-of-Shared-Transposition-Decoders/)) studied two-stage compositions `K₂ ∘ K₁` of `F`-linear class-uniform operators on `Mat_{d×d}(F^b)` with `M₁` and `M₂` column classes, using van der Waerden and Hales–Jewett arithmetic progressions to bound `d` when `K₂ ∘ K₁ = τ` and to show that `rank_F(τ - K₂ ∘ K₁) / (b d) → ∞` as `d → ∞` for fixed `(M₁, M₂)` when `(d : F) ≠ 0`. Can arithmetic progressions in that two-stage linear model be replaced by Ramsey-free additive structures with explicit bounds in `M₁`, `M₂`, and `d`, and what further dimension bounds follow in the one-stage linear shared-decoder and two-sided swap-code models of *Order, Symmetry and the Swap* ([`Order-Symmetry-and-the-Swap-A-Synthesis-of-Matrix-Transposition-on-Tapes`](../Order-Symmetry-and-the-Swap-A-Synthesis-of-Matrix-Transposition-on-Tapes/), Problems 9.1(a)–(b), Remark 5.6, and Problem 9.3) once decoder linearity and two-sided mask structure are used directly?

**Scope caveat.** No multitape Turing machine is known to induce a decomposition into one- or two-stage linear class-uniform stages. None of the results in this paper decides Statement (T) (`Transpose.LinearTimeTransposable`, linear-time multitape matrix transposition) or bridges general adaptive or oblivious Turing machines to linear shared decoders: (i) freezing genie advice or a trace cut on a finite-alphabet machine selects a *nonlinear subset* `C ⊆ Σ^{d×d}` rather than a linear subspace, and unbounded-window shared set decoders reach rate `1 - o(1)` (Theorem A.1 and Corollary A.2 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders*); (ii) at depth-`D` cone arity `Δ = b(M.k + 1)^D`, generic `O(1/Δ)` random-order deficits are exponentially smaller than cut-advice rate `ε = Ω(1/D)` (Propositions 4.13 and 4.14(c) of *Curved Motion, Depth Reduction, and Storage Codes for Matrix Transposition on Tapes*), while spine edges `u → u + 1` make per-column cut absorption have strictly negative bracket (`perColumnCutAbsorption_bracket_neg`); and (iii) curved multi-scale schedules (`Θ(log d)` velocity scales) and paired-distance-separated bounded-degree codes above half rate ([`Storage-Codes-above-Half-Rate-under-Paired-Distance-Separation`](../Storage-Codes-above-Half-Rate-under-Paired-Distance-Separation/), [`High-Rate-Separated-Storage-Codes-and-Tape-Transposition`](../High-Rate-Separated-Storage-Codes-and-Tape-Transposition/)) rule out purely graph-theoretic bounded-degree closures.

## What is proved

Every numbered theorem, lemma, proposition, corollary, and example in the paper is completely formalized and kernel-verified in Lean 4 (`v4.34.1`, `27` public modules in `BoundedArityDecoders` plus `4` shared modules in `LeanMachine.ShuffleShear`, `218` declarations audited in `PaperAxioms.lean`), depending at most on the standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` (with zero `sorry`, `admit`, `native_decide`, or custom axioms; there are no unformalized or partially formalized numbered results).

1. **Exact two-stage linear composition via affine Hilbert cubes (Section 3, Theorem 2A).**
   - Over *any* field `F`, if a subspace `W ≤ A` has an injective linear readout `L` invariant under the `2^k` ordered products `R_{k-1}^{ε_{k-1}} ⋯ R_0^{ε_0}` of `R_0, …, R_{k-1} ∈ End_F(A)`, then `(k + 1) dim_F W ≤ dim_F A + ∑_{t < k} dim_F ker(R_t - id)` (`lemma_AffineCubeCommonMessageGrowth`).
   - Extracting a monochromatic affine `M₂`-cube with steps in `{1, …, ⌊d / (2 M₂²)⌋}` by iterated Cauchy–Schwarz (`cauchySchwarzAffineCubeExtraction`) proves that for `b, M₁, M₂ ≥ 1`, `K₂ ∘ K₁ = τ` forces
     `d ≤ max(8 M₁ M₂², (2 M₁)^{2^{M₂}})` (`theorem2A_TwoStageRamseyFreeCutoff`, `theorem2A_GrowingClassesNonFactorization`).
     For fixed `M₂ ≥ 1`, this rules out exact factorizations for growing `M₁ = o(d^{1/2^{M₂}})`, including `3`-AP-free Behrend partitions with `M₁ = 2^{O(√(log d))}` (and for fixed `M₁ ≥ 1` and `ε ∈ (0, 1)`, up to `M₂ ≤ (1 - ε) log₂ log₂ d`). At small constant class counts, however, it is weaker than van der Waerden extraction: at `M₁ = M₂ = 2` it gives `d ≤ 256`, whereas Corollary 6.4 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* gives `d ≤ 16`.
   - Finite controls: on the `3`-AP-free `2`-cube `J₁₃ = {0, 1, 4, 5} ⊆ ℤ₁₃` at `q = 2`, `9 ≤ max dim_F W ≤ 10` whenever `(13 : F) ≠ 0` (`control_D13TwoCubeAPFreeSharpness`, `control_D13_GeneralFieldAndProgressionBridge`, exceeding the `≤ 8` progression bound on `4`-term APs); at `d = 4 = M₁ M₂` with `p₁ = p₂ = [0, 0, 1, 1]`, entry-copy and bit-lane-copy rules fail for all `b ≥ 1` over every alphabet `|Σ| ≥ 2`, while coded linear factorizations `K₂ ∘ K₁ = τ` exist for all lane widths `b ≥ 0` over every field with `(5 : F) ≠ 0` (`control_D4SameIntervalCodedFactorization`, `control_D4_AllWidthsAndBinaryFields`; characteristic `5` for `b ≥ 1` is open).

2. **Approximate two-stage linear composition via shifted sumsets (Section 4, Theorem 2B).**
   - Over any field `F` with `(d : F) ≠ 0`, combining minimum-shifted `(2 M₂)`-fold monochromatic sumsets, arbitrary-subset Vandermonde pullbacks on `SharedDecoders.bandRowSpace`, sparse annihilating polynomials, and iterated Kővári–Sós–Turán extraction proves that for all `M₁, M₂ ≥ 1` and all `d ≥ D₀(M₁, M₂)`,
     `rank_F(τ - K₂ ∘ K₁) ≥ c(M₁, M₂) · b · d · (log d)^{1 / (2 M₂ - 1)}`
     (`theorem2B_TwoStageLogPowerRankDeficit`, `theorem2B_ExplicitConstant`), and `Ω(b d log d)` when `M₂ = 1` (`theorem2B_OneSecondStage_OmegaLog`).
   - Explicit constants: `c(M₁, M₂) = 1 / (144 · (log(16 M₁ M₂))^{1/(2M₂ - 1)})` and `D₀(M₁, M₂) = max(4 M₂, (16 M₁ M₂)^{36^{2 M₂ - 1}} + 2)`; in particular `D₀(1, 1) = 16^{36} + 2 ≈ 2.23 × 10^{43}`, so the bound is purely asymptotic and requires `(d : F) ≠ 0`.
   - At `d = 12, k = 2`, an explicit `24`-dimensional `4`-channel witness (`q = 4`, `b M₂ = 2`) over `ℂ` on `J₁₂ = {0, 1, 3, 4, 7, 9}` shows that relaxing stage-1 shift covariance to an arbitrary linear readout on a single `k = 2` band admits `24 = 12 k` dimensions (`control_D12SingleClassAbstractBand`, `control_D12_BandRowSpaceChannelEquiv`).

3. **Run-extension bounds for one-stage linear shared decoders (Section 5, Problem 9.1(a)).**
   - Evaluating an `F`-linear decoder on `rot_s(X) ∈ 𝒳` at the right endpoint of each maximal monochromatic cyclic run along each orbit `O_a` of `c ↦ c + s` (`0 ≤ a < g_s = gcd(d, s)`, length `n = d / g_s`) yields an extra linear equation per run beyond the nonlinear link-covariance space `H_{{0, s}}`. Filtering by `rot_s`-eigenmodes over `F̄` proves that whenever `char(F) ∤ n` (`extension_Theorem1A_SingleShift`, `linearClassUniform_singleShift_finrank_le`),
     `dim_F(𝒳 ∩ rot_s⁻¹(𝒳)) ≤ B_run(b, p, s) := b g_s ∑_{a < g_s} ∑_{m < M} (T_{m,a}(n - d_{m,a}) - T_{m,a}²/2 + T_{m,a}/2) + b g_s d`
     (where `d_{m,a} = |J_m ∩ O_a|` and `T_{m,a} = |{c ∈ J_m ∩ O_a : c + s ∉ J_m}|`). On the alternating two-coloring `p(c) = c mod 2` for even `d = 2m ≥ 2` at `b = 1, s = 1`, an explicit `2m(m + 1)`-dimensional linear class-uniform code over *every* field (including `char(F) = 2`) attains `dim_F(𝒳 ∩ rot_1⁻¹(𝒳)) = m² + 3m = d²/4 + 3d/2 = B_run(1, p, 1)` with equality, vs. `dim_F H_{{0, 1}} = d²` (`extension_Theorem1A_AlternatingSharpness`, `alternatingSharpCode_finrank_and_shift_inf`).
   - For two classes (`|κ| ≤ 2`), Cauchy–Schwarz across the `g_s` orbits gives `dim_F(𝒳 ∩ rot_s⁻¹(𝒳)) ≤ b(Q_s d / 2 - Q_s² / 4 + g_s Q_s / 2 + g_s d)` (`Q_s = |{c ∈ ℤ_d : p(c) ≠ p(c + s)}|`), and averaging across shifts proves `D_b(p) ≤ (19/32) b d² + (3/2) b d^{3/2}` whenever `char(F) ∤ d` (`extension_Theorem1B_TwoClass`, `linearClassUniform_twoClass_finrank_le`), strictly below both the `41/64` bound of Theorem 3.4 and the `5/8` nested-component nonlinear-decoder certificate floor of Theorem C.6 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* (while above the `9/16` full-ambient floor of Theorem C.7; positive characteristics dividing `d`—where Mahler binomial polynomials `binom(c, j) mod p` exceed root-count bounds—remain open). At prime `d ≠ char(F)` and `M ≥ 3`, shift averaging proves `D_b(p) ≤ c_M b d² + b M d` with `c_M = 1 - 5/(4M) + 1/M² - 1/(4M³)` (`extension_Theorem1B_MultiClassPrime`, `linearClassUniform_multiClass_prime_finrank_le`), whose leading coefficient beats the flag bound `1 - 1/(2M)` for every `M ≥ 2` (`c_3 = 37/54 < 5/6`, `c_4 = 191/256 < 7/8`) and improves on Theorem 3.5 of *Capacity and Composition of Shared Transposition Decoders* (`1 - 1/(4⌈√(2M)⌉)`) on primes for `2 ≤ M ≤ 49` and `51 ≤ M ≤ 59`, whereas Theorem 3.5 has a stronger `Θ(M^{-1/2})` deficit at `M = 50` and `M ≥ 60` and applies to composite `d` and arbitrary characteristic.

4. **Exact dimension `25` for `{0, 1, 3} | {2, 4, 5}` on `ℤ₆` outside characteristics `2` and `3` (Section 6, Problem 9.1(b)).**
   - While Theorem 1A alone gives `≤ 26` on `{0, 1, 3} | {2, 4, 5}`, coupled six-mode column-Fourier analysis on `H_{{0, 1}}`, an antipodal quotient inequality `3 dim Q + S_K ≤ 24`, and kernel-disjointness / `4 × 4` row elimination (determinant `16/3`, or `2⁴ · 3³` after clearing `1/3`) prove `dim_F(𝒳 ∩ rot_1⁻¹(𝒳)) ≤ 14` (`aperiodicSix_shift_inf_finrank_le_14`) and hence `D₁({0, 1, 3}, {2, 4, 5}) = 25` (for `SwapRigidity.aperiodicCls` and any equivalent two-class coloring `IsAperiodicSixPartition`) over every field with `char(F) ∉ {2, 3}` (`aperiodicSix_finrank_le_25`, `aperiodicSix_maxDim_eq_25`, `extension_Theorem2_AperiodicSixExact`, paired with Theorem 3.2 / `ClassUniformMemo.exists_code_of_three_ne_zero` of [`memo-class-uniform-codes`](../Order-Symmetry-and-the-Swap-A-Synthesis-of-Matrix-Transposition-on-Tapes/memo-class-uniform-codes/)). Characteristics `2` (`25 ≤ D₁ ≤ 28`, where `Z⁶ - 1 = (Z³ - 1)²`) and `3` (`24 ≤ D₁ ≤ 28`, where `Z⁶ - 1 = (Z² - 1)³`, Problem 6.1 of [`memo-class-uniform-codes`](../Order-Symmetry-and-the-Swap-A-Synthesis-of-Matrix-Transposition-on-Tapes/memo-class-uniform-codes/)) remain open.

5. **Three-term swap codes and one- and two-sided interval-mask bounds (Section 7, Remark 5.6 and Problem 9.3).**
   - Over every field `F`, the `3 × 3` block family `∑_{t=0}^2 A_t X B_tᵀ = Xᵀ` on `d = 3 m` has dimension `4 m (m + 1)` (`bonus1_ThreeTermBlockSwapCodeDim`), and diagonal tail padding achieves `29 > 28` at `d = 7` and `11 > 10` at `d = 4` (`bonus1_ThreeTermPaddedSevenAndFive`, `bonus1_UnifiedThreeTermExceedsPairingBound`), giving three-term swap codes over every field exceeding `d(d+1)/2` at `d ∈ {6, 7, 9, 12}` and uniformly at `d = 4` (alongside `d = 3`, extending Remark 5.6 of *Order, Symmetry and the Swap*).
   - For multi-scale cyclic-interval masks, one-sided endpoint refinement gives `2 dim_F swapCode(A, B) ≤ d² + d min(β_A, β_B)` (`bonus2_MultiscaleIntervalBothSides`, `bonus2_SublinearProfileAndTendstoHalf`), which uses only one side's sparsity and becomes vacuous once `min(β_A, β_B) ≥ d`. Over *every* field `F`, decomposing side `A` into a fine union `S_A` (`|S_A| = s_A`, `m_A = ṁ(S_A)` or `m(S_A)` cyclic runs) plus `c_A` coarse intervals partitioning `L_A = ℤ_d \ S_A` (and `(S_B, m_B, c_B)` on side `B`) proves `dim_F swapCode(A, B) ≤ min(B_cf(d; A, B), B_cf(d; B, A))` where `B_cf(d; A, B) = d²/2 + s_B(d - s_A)/4 + s_A s_B/2 + c_B d/2 + c_A s_B/4 + s_B m_A/2 + m_B(d - s_A)/4` (`extension_Theorem3_TwoSidedCoarseFine`, `swapCode_finrank_le_twoSidedCoarseFine`). On contiguous fine singletons plus one coarse complement (`c_A = c_B = m_A = m_B = 1`), this gives `(1/2 + (1/4) min(β(1 + α), α(1 + β))) d² + (5/4) d`, strictly beating one-sided Theorem TP's leading coefficient `1/2 + (1/2) min(α, β)` for all `α, β ∈ (0, 1)` (`extension_Theorem3_ConcreteWinOverTP`, `swapCode_finrank_le_contiguousCoarseFine`, yielding `(11/16) d² + d < (3/4) d²` at `α = β = 1/2` for all even `d ≥ 18`; a numerical remark notes that small-grid SVD checks over `ℝ` at `d ∈ {5, 6, 7, 8}` matched `⌊(d² + ∑_{i,j} min(|I_i|, |J_j|))/2⌋`, i.e. `(5/8) d² + O(d)` at `α = β = 1/2`, not used in proofs). Periodic Walsh digit masks have `2^{j+1}` cyclic boundaries, profile `2` per scale, and operator span dimension `≤ (m + 1) 2^m` (`walshDigitMask_in_periodicDyadicSpan`, `bonus2_WalshDichotomyStructuralProperties`).

6. **Restricted shuffle-and-local operator pipelines, verified machines in `lean-machine`, and anti-diagonal untwisting (Section 8).**
   - Across `F^d_row ⊗ (F^d_col ⊗ F^b)`, `rk_⊗(τ) = d²` and `rk_⊗(S) = rk_⊗(S⁻¹) = 4` on `d = 2^m ≥ 2`, so any pipeline of `S, S⁻¹`, `I_d ⊗ B`, and `A ⊗ I_{db}` realizing `τ` uses `t ≥ m = log₂ d` shuffle/unshuffle passes (`operatorSchmidtRankSwap`, `operatorSchmidtRankStoneShuffle`, `stoneShufflePipelinePassLowerBound`, `boundedOSRPipelinePassLowerBound`).
   - In [`../lean-machine/LeanMachine/ShuffleShear/`](../lean-machine/LeanMachine/ShuffleShear/) (transported to `Transpose.TM` in `ShuffleShearBridge.lean`), Stone's shuffle (`3` work tapes, `≤ 150 L` steps), Stone's unshuffle (`2` work tapes, `≤ 150 L` steps), and within-row cyclic shear (`2` work tapes, `≤ 200 L` steps) are verified in linear time (`stoneShuffleTM_threeTape_linear`, `stoneUnshuffleTM_twoTape_linear`, `rowShearTM_twoTape_linear`). Because within-row shear runs in `O(L)` time yet has `rk_⊗ = d` (`control_RowShearAndRowDiagonalAlternation`, `rowDiagonal_SourceArityGaugeInvariance`), `rk_⊗` does *not* answer Problem 9.5 of *Order, Symmetry and the Swap*.
   - Discrete Shannon mutual information satisfies `I(W_{r,c}; X_{:,c'}) = 0` on `W = Xᵀ ⊕ X` (`skewOneTimePad_ShannonMutualInfoZero`), and anti-diagonal coordinates `Z_{c, a} = X_{c, a - c}` intertwine `rot_t` with `id ⊗ σ^t` and factor shift-link covariance as `U_E(p) ⊗ (ℤ_d → A)` (`pairwiseShiftCollisionCharacterization`, `antiDiagonalTensorSeparation`, `antiDiagTensorEquiv_intertwines_rot`, `shiftSetCovariance_tensor_finrank`, `maskFloor_GenericSubspaceRealization`).

7. **Cut-causal cone evaluation, the `1/(Δ+1)` random-order bound, and why column classes cannot absorb per-entry cut labels (Section 9).**
   - On frozen trace/cut fibres of `Transpose.TM`, depth-`D` `CausalConeTree`s evaluated via `M.δ` reproduce the live run on explicit syntactic cone footprints of size `≤ (M.k + 1)^D` per bit (`tmTraceCutConeDetermination`, `evalConeTree_cellSupport`, `evalConeTree_traceConeTree_eq`).
   - `randomOrder_BoundedAritySetCapacity` formalizes in Lean the `(d² - |ℰ| - d M Δ)/(Δ + 1)` random-order acyclic-subgraph bound of Proposition 4.14(a)–(b) of *Curved Motion, Depth Reduction, and Storage Codes for Matrix Transposition on Tapes* (after Bar-Yossef–Birk–Jayram–Kol), alongside boundary constructions at `Δ = d`, `2d - 1`, and `d²` (`boundaryControl_DeficitVanishesAtDeltaEqD`).
   - `perColumnCutAbsorption_bracket_neg` proves that on any nonempty depth-`D` frozen fibre, spine causal edges `u → u + 1` make `d² - |ℰ| - d M₀ Δ < 0` both when cut-touching outputs are discarded and when column classes are refined by `L_cut` per-column cut slots (complementing Proposition 4.14(c)'s `1/D` vs. `1/Δ` cut-cost barrier), explaining why bridging periodic stretches requires the per-position fixed-alphabet form of Problem 9.4(b) of *Order, Symmetry and the Swap*.

## What is open

- Statement (T) (`Transpose.LinearTimeTransposable`, linear-time multitape matrix transposition) and the Tape Coding Conjecture.
- Upgrading Theorem 2B from `Ω(b d (log d)^{1/(2 M₂ - 1)})` to `Ω_{M₁, M₂}(b d²)` and extending it to `char(F) ∣ d` (including `𝔽₂` at `d = 2^m`), as well as characteristic `5` for `b ≥ 1` in the `d = 4` `[0,0,1,1]` factorization.
- Lowering the one-stage two-class linear ceiling `(19/32) b d² + (3/2) b d^{3/2}` to `(1/2 + o(1)) b d²`, extending linear run-extension bounds to `char(F) ∣ d` and composite `d` for `M ≥ 3` (Problem 9.1(a) of *Order, Symmetry and the Swap*), and determining `D₁({0, 1, 3}, {2, 4, 5})` on `ℤ₆` in characteristics `2` (`25 ≤ D₁ ≤ 28`) and `3` (`24 ≤ D₁ ≤ 28`, Problem 9.1(b) and Problem 6.1 of [`memo-class-uniform-codes`](../Order-Symmetry-and-the-Swap-A-Synthesis-of-Matrix-Transposition-on-Tapes/memo-class-uniform-codes/)).
- Three-term swap codes exceeding `d(d+1)/2` for `d = 5` and for `d ≥ 8` outside `{9, 12}` (Remark 5.6 of *Order, Symmetry and the Swap*), sharp two-sided interval partition bounds (`⌊(d² + ∑_{i,j} min(|I_i|, |J_j|))/2⌋`), and nontrivial swap-code upper bounds when both sides lie in the `(m + 1) 2^m`-dimensional periodic Walsh-masked rotation span (Problem 9.3 of *Order, Symmetry and the Swap*).
- Fixed-alphabet `1/2 + o(1)` rate bounds at fixed windows `w ≥ 3` with a separate recovery function for each position (Problem 9.4(b) of *Order, Symmetry and the Swap*), machine reductions from adaptive schedules (Problem 9.4(a)), and multi-scale subadditive machine invariants unaffected by single linear-time operations such as within-row shear (Problem 9.5).

## Building and verifying

Clone the entire repository so relative Lake paths (`../../Capacity-and-Composition-of-Shared-Transposition-Decoders/lean`, `../../Order-Symmetry-and-the-Swap-A-Synthesis-of-Matrix-Transposition-on-Tapes/memo-class-uniform-codes/lean`, and `../../lean-machine`) resolve. Lean 4 and Mathlib are pinned to `v4.34.1`.

First, build and audit the shared shuffle/unshuffle/shear machines in `../lean-machine`:

```sh
cd ../lean-machine
lake exe cache get
lake build
lake env lean Axioms.lean
```

Then, from this paper's directory, regenerate the declaration index and axiom audit, build the Lean library, check axioms, and compile the PDF:

```sh
python3 tools/lean_index.py
cd lean
lake exe cache get
lake build
lake env lean PaperAxioms.lean
cd ../paper
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
```
