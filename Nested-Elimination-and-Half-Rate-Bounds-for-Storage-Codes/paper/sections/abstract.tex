Can a deterministic multitape Turing machine transpose a $d\times d$ matrix of $\lceil\log_2d\rceil$-bit entries in time
linear in the input length? Earlier papers of this series reduce the impossibility of oblivious linear-time transposers to
depth reduction and an all-code question about storage codes: on bounded-degree recovery relations without short paired
paths, can a storage code beat rate~$\frac12$? We prove the exact half bound $2h(V)\le\sum_vh(\{v\})$ for every valid
polymatroid, and hence $\card C^2\le\card\alpha^{\card V}$ for every storage code, linear or not, over every alphabet, on
large classes of recovery relations, most of them defined by the absence of transitive triangles. The engine is a nested
elimination lemma (Lemma~E), which extends the inequality form of an exact partition identity of the series' circular
memo by outside parents with nested charges, and a decomposition theorem (Theorem~D) that composes certified pieces. Its
instances include triangle-free graphs of maximum degree three (Theorem~N3); loopless relations without transitive
triangles whose underlying graph has maximum degree three (Theorem~U3), or whose vertices of in-degree at least three
induce an acyclic sub-relation, with no degree bound (Theorem~T); loopless relations partitioned into blocks with private
outside parents, each block chordless subcubic, a chorded digon path without transitive triangles, or internally
certified (Theorem~PC); and every
Cayley relation $\mathrm{Cay}(G,\{s,s^{-1},t\})$ without transitive triangles, for every finite group, when
$\{s,s^{-1},t\}$ has three elements and does not contain~$1$ (Corollary~CC). On triangle-free graphs of maximum degree three
the guessing number equals the fractional matching number, and the broadcast rate of index coding is the number of vertices
minus it. We also show where the method stops: in a $15$-vertex relation with in- and out-degrees at most three the unique
minimum nonempty core admits no certificate, some chorded cycles admit no valid sweep, the Petersen graph admits no global
certificate, and on the folded cubes, whose odd girth equals their degree, the half bound fails, so that a half bound
assuming only the absence of short paired paths up to a fixed length must restrict the degree. The numbered results are
formalized in Lean~4, except for the step from the block form to the strongly-connected-component form of
Corollary~$T_{\mathrm{int}}$, the digon-component form of Theorem~PC, a paid-deletion corollary, a lemma and two
propositions of a few lines, and the two remarks that pass from the formal code bounds to the guessing number and the
broadcast rate, all of which have complete written proofs; the computations about the $15$-vertex relation, the
chorded cycles and the Petersen graph are numerical remarks, and the folded-cube formula is cited. These are unconditional
storage-code theorems: they constrain constructions and locate the remaining work for an impossibility proof, but they do
not decide whether linear-time transposition is possible.
