   alphabet. It copies P bits forwards to scratch in2P steps, rewinds the

   The actual schedule has exactly6P+2 steps, also for P=0. Starting from
   arbitrary work-head coordinates, bank contents, finite mode and IO,

      bank[origin+j] = data(bit(original bank[origin+P-1-j])), j<P.

   bank cell outside [origin,origin+P) is preserved. The last preservation
   fact supports later local row operations. All copies and resets are

   the6P+2-step stage, from arbitrary starting configurations. The bank
