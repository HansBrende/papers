WHY THESE ARE COHERENT
Each large group represents a complete routine and its obligations: machine,
calls, execution, semantics, resource bound, and (where relevant) cleanup or
geometry. For example RecordedReusableRow includes preparation, execution,
cleanup and output recording. PaidPaddingMachine includes generation, safety,
preparation, cleanup, payload geometry and the final resource limits. These
are not arbitrary file-count buckets. Source namespaces and all nested section
boundaries remain in the exact original per-file bodies, ordered topologically.
The largest group is still under 2,400 lines.

MULTIPLE CONSUMERS AND CYCLE CHECK
The conservative 37-group variant required each intermediate to have only the
next member as its sole mathematical direct consumer. That safely removed 160
boundaries, but left a NativeMicrocode rebuild path of 92 nodes. The broader
plan additionally absorbs full Data/Resources/Geometry branches and remaps
intermediate consumers to the final module. Every individual group contraction
is acyclic and the combined quotient is acyclic. The complete quotient edges
are included in the JSON, not inferred merely from the endpoint path.
The quotient check is performed over all 1,157 local mathematical modules;
SupportAxioms root imports are listed separately and must be remapped too.
No original sibling-paper import is changed.

Acyclicity establishes there is no dependency path between two merged members
that leaves their combined group and returns through an excluded intermediate.
It does not by itself establish unchanged elaboration: new external imports
are available earlier in a concatenated file, so exact declaration-type
comparison, ordinary Lean proof checking and full axiom auditing are essential.

SOURCE / INSTANCE PRESERVATION
All original source hashes are recorded, with each member's exact mathematical
consumers, external imports, root-audit imports, scope structure and options.
All selected namespace/section stacks balance. The scope scan found no private,
local-attribute, scoped-notation or syntax-extension declarations. The only
set_option lines found are autoImplicit false, retained verbatim.

This scan does not eliminate generated-name concerns. The independent
preservation review found anonymous instances in seven broad groups; their
class type/predicate constants are project-local. Installed Lean's naming rule
therefore does not introduce a new module-root suffix for these moves. That
narrows the risk but does not replace the full audited-type baseline/diff and
ordinary build. Deriving-generated declarations should likewise be covered by
normal elaboration plus the exact audit-name/type comparisons.

It preserves every non-import source byte and all 6,297 audit commands, and
finds no missing local imports or cycles. The exact candidate's normal build
and post-build declaration-type/axiom checks remain root-owned work.

MEASUREMENT / ROLLOUT
The five-file SourceEndpoints experiment remains the first measured gate.
For one larger zero-explicit-instance test, RecordedReusableRow is a natural
2,379-line routine and removes 19 serial file boundaries. A bounded four-group
batch (RecordedReusableRow, FamilyReader, PageAndVertexScheduler,
PaidPaddingMachine) reduces the NativeMicrocode path 176 -> 121; its usefulness
is testing larger bodies, not exhausting the available speedup.

The strongest follow-on is the broad19 candidate plus the independently
validated import refactor, rather than dozens of repeated full builds after
small source moves. Root should capture exact declaration types first, apply
one archived transformation, run targeted ordinary builds, then the full build
and all-declaration axiom/type audit. Preserve the matching before/after
controlled rebuild timing workload. Do not claim the graph ratios as wall-time
ratios, and do not clear caches or rewrite artifact traces to manufacture gains.

Maintainability tradeoff: editing an early definition in a complete routine
now also rechecks its later declarations within that file. A former light
intermediate importer may acquire later routine prerequisites. These costs
are explicit in the quotient graph, whose cycle check alone cannot decide
whether an individual file has become inconvenient. Keep the original source
archive and group inventory so a particular module boundary can be restored
if measured or editorial use warrants it.
