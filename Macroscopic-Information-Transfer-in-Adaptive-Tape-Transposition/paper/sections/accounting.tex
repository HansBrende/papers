\section{Joint information and exact cut records}\label{sec:accounting}

The following consequences make explicit what is counted jointly. They
do not justify adding the information of a reused record across times,
rectangles or scales. All entropies in this section are in bits.

\begin{lemma}[Finite joint invoices]\label{lem:joint-invoices}
\formal{InformationAccounting.lean}{joint_record_entropy_identity,joint_record_information_lower,joint_record_card_bound}
On any finite probability space, if $x\mapsto(M(x),E(x),R(x),Z(x))$
is injective, then
\[
 H(Z\mid M,E,R)=H(x)-H(M)-H(E\mid M)-H(R\mid M,E).
\]
Upper bounds on the last two conditional terms give the corresponding
lower bound on the import term. Separately, an injective map from a finite
set $S$ to an $e$-bit exterior and a record $C$ satisfies
$|S|\le2^e|\operatorname{range}_S C|$; only the attained joint range is
paid.
\end{lemma}
\begin{outline}
An injective deterministic observation has the entropy of its source.
Expand conditional entropy and telescope the three nested pair entropies.
The imported library uses natural logarithms; division by $\log 2$ gives
the displayed bit convention. For cardinality, pair the exterior with
the record viewed in the finite type of its attained image, and apply
cardinality monotonicity to this injection. Constructing a native record
with that injectivity property remains a separate obligation.
The metadata-dependent support form is also checked in
\lean{conditional_entropy_le_mean_log_support} and
\lean{joint_import_information_from_support}: a menu of possible record
values at each metadata value costs the expected logarithm of that menu's
cardinality. Empty and zero-mass metadata fibres contribute zero. This
proves the variable-length entropy step without assuming every metadata
fibre has positive mass.
\end{outline}

\begin{corollary}[Conditional information in the import record]\label{cor:import-information}
\formal{NativeImportInformation.lean}{NativeReplayClass.code_injective,NativeReplayFamily.native_import_information_saving}
Give a nonempty timely correct set $S$ its uniform law and make the choices
of \cref{thm:replay} deterministically. Let $Z_0$ be its geometry metadata,
$E$ the exterior raw word, $R_0$ the ordinary state/window/slab records,
and $Z$ the single tagged import record. Put $D=P-\log_2|S|$. Then
\begin{align*}
 H(Z\mid Z_0,E,R_0)
 &\ge\mathbb E|U|-D-H(Z_0)-H(R_0\mid Z_0,E)\\
 &\ge\mathbb E[|U|-b_0]-D-H(Z_0),\\
 b_0&=a\mathbf1_{a_0>0}W+a(k-1)r(2H+1)+rq.
\end{align*}
Here $r,U,a_0$ are the selected input-dependent geometric quantities.
More generally the same two bounds hold under any one probability law on
$S$, with $D=P-H(X)$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{outline}
For each metadata value the checked class contains actual execution prefixes,
entry observations, extrema, output counters and the computed import record.
The canonical run decomposition and counter intervals give the replay
hypotheses. The native replay theorem therefore proves the record injection;
injectivity is not a field assumed by the class interface.

Apply the finite entropy identity to the metadata-indexed family of these
actual records. Conditional on $Z_0$, the exterior has a fixed coordinate
set and at most $P-|U|$ bits, so
$H(E\mid Z_0)\le P-\mathbb E|U|$. Rearranging gives the first inequality.
At each fixed metadata value the ordinary record has support at most
$2^{b_0}$, yielding the second after conditioning on $E$ and averaging.
Finite dependent record types are tagged by the metadata already in the
conditioning variables; they incur no extra choice cost. All terms use the
same law, and $Z$ includes tags as well as values. The formal statement uses
natural logarithms and permits arbitrary finite free cell sets and demand
maps. Dividing by $\log 2$, specializing to the slab, and using
$H(X)=\log_2|S|$ for the uniform law gives the displayed version.
\end{outline}

\begin{proposition}[Exact frozen-trace cut invoice]\label{prop:frozen-invoice}
\formal{FrozenCutInvoice.lean}{native_cut_recovers,native_frozen_full_invoice,native_frozen_field_invoice,native_frozen_cut_entropy_lower}
Let a nonempty correct set $S$ share one complete head/append trace through
the relevant halt horizon. Let $Q_0$ be $D_0$ distinct demanded raw
coordinates, $R$ their designated correct append roots, and let $\Gamma$
be an endpoint-inclusive cut from $R$ to every actual read of $Q_0$.
If $C_\Gamma$ is the joint full step-value record at the cut, then
\[
 D_0-(P-\log_2|S|)
 \le\log_2|\operatorname{range}_S C_\Gamma|
 \le(q+ka+2)|\Gamma|.
\]
If only post-state and written work symbols are retained at every cut
step, together with the emitted bit at roots in $\Gamma$, the sharper
field bill is
\[
 D_0-(P-\log_2|S|)\le(q+ka)|\Gamma|+|\Gamma\cap R|.
\]
The statement holds for any fixed output permutation and any entry width.
Moreover, under any one probability law on $S$, the same joint full record
satisfies
\[
 H(C_\Gamma\mid X|_{[0,P)\setminus Q_0})
 \ge H(X)-(P-D_0).
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{outline}
Give the decoder $X$ outside $Q_0$ and the cut record. A cut root's bit
is in its full value. For any uncut root, follow all its uncut ancestors
in the fixed dependency graph. Every such vertex reads outside $Q_0$,
since otherwise its ancestor path contradicts separation. Reconstruct
these vertices in increasing time. Each earlier state/work parent is
either retained and already reconstructed, cut and supplied, or initial
and known. The complete common old tuple determines the instruction and
output. Latest work visits, and all physical positions and append ordinals,
are fixed by the trace. Hence all demanded output bits, and therefore
$X$ on $Q_0$, are recovered. This proves an injection into the exterior
bits and the \emph{joint range} of the cut record, giving the first bound.
Its product alphabet gives the second.

For the field version, retained descendants need only a cut predecessor's
post-state and work writes. They never read its output. Only a cut desired
root needs its own emitted bit; its append flag is already fixed by the
trace. Count those fields to obtain the sharper formula. Its root term
cannot be discarded: a cut root can emit its raw input bit while keeping
all state and work fields constant. A single encoder pooling different
privately selected traces or cuts across inputs must charge those choices;
this formula does not remove the geometry menu from \cref{thm:input-cut}.
Applying the universal frozen-fibre inequality separately to each fixed
cut needs no additional cut-choice bill, as in
\cref{prop:eroded-input-transfer}.

The checked statement uses arbitrary input length and designated roots
whose actual native output bits equal the demanded coordinates. It proves
the reconstruction from the actual field-level transition recursion and
the literal endpoint-inclusive dependency-path separator. Its trace
agreement is required only through the horizon, so the complete common
trace in the proposition is sufficient. Neither injectivity nor cone
determination is an additional premise.
For the arbitrary-law statement, the proved exterior/record injection
identifies their joint entropy with $H(X)$. Subtract the exterior's entropy,
which is at most $P-D_0$ bits. This uses the native reconstruction just
proved, not a further hypothesis of injectivity.
\end{outline}
