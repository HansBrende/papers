  E_w[length] >= P - E_w[separatorLoss] - 2(P-H_w(X)) - 2H_w(helper).
All entropies in this displayed formula are bits. The source uses natural logarithms divided by log 2.

when E_w[length] <= (1-epsilon)P and n,b>0.

Core proof:

     H(w) <= H(f_*w) + sum_a (f_*w)(a) log|f^{-1}(a)|.
   This holds without normalization and uses no chain-rule axiom. Every attained fiber is nonempty.
