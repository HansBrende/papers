EXACT STATEMENTS AND DEPENDENCY MAP
VERIFIED FINITE ALGEBRAIC ENDPOINT
Let j range over any finite type, with s_j a nonzero real number and b_j real. Let delta>0. There exists a nonempty finite family F of invertible real affine maps such that, simultaneously for every j, writing H_j={f composed with (x -> s_j x+b_j):f in F},

  |F triangle H_j| <= delta |F union H_j|.

Lean represents a map by (a,t) in R^units x R. Its explicit right composition is (a,t) -> (a s_j,t+a b_j). affineRight_apply verifies the action formula; affineRight_injective proves this composition preserves distinct maps. The endpoint is exists_affine_small_symmetric_difference. It permits negative slopes, dependent/duplicate generators and an empty generator family. The family F and its size depend on the requested maps and tolerance; no uniform size or computational bound is claimed.

PROOF COMPONENTS
1. exists_small_growth_step: A real sequence with a(0)>=1 and a(n)<=(n+1)^m has some a(n+1)<=r a(n), for each r>1. Otherwise a(n)>=r^n contradicts mathlib isLittleO_pow_const_const_pow_of_one_lt. No fresh proof of the analytic asymptotic theorem.
2. exponentBox g n is the image of exponent vectors with coordinates in{0,...,n}, under e -> product_i g_i^e_i, for any finite family in a commutative group. Its size is at most(n+1)^m even with arbitrary relations among the generators. It is nonempty, nested and contains each generator translate of the previous box.
3. exists_small_mul_boundary: For every epsilon>0, there is one nonempty finite F with |F g_j minus F|<=epsilon|F| for all j. exists_small_add_boundary reuses the SAME proof through Multiplicative, rather than copying it for additive groups.
4. affine_rectangle_escape_card: For finite slopes S and translations T, the number of maps leaving S x T under right composition is at most |{a in S:a s notin S}| |T| + sum_(a in S) |{t in T:t+a b notin T}|. The proof uses injective image/filter counting, product cardinality and mathlib's finite-union bound.
5. Choose S with slope boundary at most epsilon/2. Then choose T with additive boundary at most epsilon/2 simultaneously for all shifts a b_j indexed by S x J. Their rectangle has one-sided boundary at most epsilon|S||T|. Equal cardinalities give equal one-sided differences. Applying epsilon=delta/2 and |F|<=|F union H_j| proves the endpoint.
